# Air forces

Code: `crates/opensow-formats/src/air.rs` (`AirForce`, `AirStock`, `Level::air_stock`). Test: `tests/real_data.rs::air_force_stock_*`.

## The five forces
| code | force | sprites (`specials\`) | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | Bomber | `bomber_l/r` (3 frames each), bombs `bomba_l/r` (9), `bomba_ex` | |
| 31 | Carryall | `carryAll` (5 rotor frames), `carryAll_down`, `carryAll_rear` | long transport; the "reinforcement" aircraft (unconfirmed) |
| 32 | Fighter | `fighter` (1 frame, a jet), `fighter_smoke`, `fighter_bomb`, `fighter_ex` | manual: immune to anti-air units and turrets (8000 HP); "Fighter Assault" |
| 33 | Tripler | `tripler` (10 frames, three-pod craft), `tripler_ph` (20) | "Tripler Strafe" |
| 34 | Asteroid | `meteor_medium/small` + `_aura` + `_smoke` (16 frames each) | no aircraft: a meteor strike |

## How it was decoded
- Aircraft draw routine `0x467240` indexes `[obj+0x28] - 30` (0..3) into bomber / carryall / **fighter (32, one frame, `0x5ea3a4`)** / **tripler (33, ten frames, `0x5ea328`)** sprite arrays (an earlier version of these notes had 32/33 swapped); code 34 only appears with the meteor sprites (`0x410xxx` cluster).
- Speech events 17-21 (`0x403340` switch) = bomber, carryall, asteroid, fighter, tripler "ready" (strings 136-140; a different order than the codes). Icon tooltips are strings 200-204: Bomber Attack, Meteor Storm, CarryAll, Tripler Strafe, Fighter Assault (= header order 30, 34, 31, 33, 32).
- Icon/panel order is 30, 34, 31, 33, 32 (switch at `0x416d00`) and the level header uses the same order.

## Level header: starting stock
For group `g` in (Bomber, Asteroid, Carryall, Tripler, Fighter) = codes 30, 34, 31, 33, 32 (the loader queues exactly this order) the loader `0x41d88e..0x41d9bd` reads header word **`5+3g`** for the player (block `0x70ba50`) and **`6+3g`** for the enemy (block `0x70c6b8`); word `4+3g` is never read and is 0 in every shipped file. (An earlier version of this note and of `Level::air_stock` used `4+3g` as the player and `5+3g` as the enemy, which gave the campaign's stock to the wrong side.)
- Campaign (`.edt`): only the player's words are set. M10 gives the **player** 4 bombers, 7 carryalls, 4 triplers and 6 fighters (credits 0, research 400 for the enemy), M05 10 fighters, M04/M06 6 fighters. The enemy starts with none and earns its forces from its air bases.
- Multiplayer (`.net`): both words are set to the same count (Island of Fate: 12 fighters each, Ice Blizzard: 3 carryalls + 2 fighters each), which is how the layout was confirmed; `multiplayer.rs` already used it.

## Behaviour (`aircraft.rs`)
Aircraft are objects in per-side arrays (`0x5ba910`, 30 slots per side, counts `0x575758`) drawn sorted by y (`0x420280`). Launch `0x467ee0` (called from the air-strike click handlers, lockstep RNG slot via `0x43a9b0`) -> `Aircraft::launch`, verified field by field against the exe (`opensow launch`, 300 random cases); the per-tick update is `0x467b50` (per-type cases at jump table `0x467ec4`), death `0x467850`, spawns `0x468240`, carryall unload `0x468960`. Ported from reading, not emulation-verified: `Aircraft::step`. OpenSOW: `World::step_aircraft` (`sim/air_strikes.rs`) runs it once per tick for every aircraft; launches go through `World::launch_strike`.
- Common: speed 120 (steps per 60 ticks, `projectile::steps_at`), altitude 200, death timer 180. Enter from the map edge opposite the target: target x > 1024 -> from the right (x = map width + 400, flying left), else from the left (x = -400). Removed past x = -400 / width + 400, or after the death timer once HP <= 0.
- HP / frames: bomber 480 (3 frames, 3 ticks each), carryall 360, tripler 270 (10 frames, 5 ticks each), fighter 8000 (altitude 120, always from the left, 12 px per tick = 720 px/s), asteroid 120 (no aircraft: x 1000, y -8000).
- **Bomber (30)**: slows near the target: speed 120 minus 0/15/30/40/50/60 when |dx| to the target is >= 190 / < 190 / < 180 / < 170 / < 160 / < 150. While `|x + reach - target_x| < 150` (reach = -45 flying left, +45 flying right) it drops a bomb every `rand(2)+4` ticks: bullet type 18, strength 110, from (x+dx, y-altitude+dy) to (x+reach+dx, y+dy) with dx in -13..12, dy in -20..19 (carpet bombing, ~60 bombs per pass).
- **Fighter (32)**: two salvos per pass, each two type-26 missiles (strength 150, speed 150) at x >= target_x - 226 and x >= target_x - 182: from (x-4, y-alt+22) to (x+204, y-8) and from (x, y-alt+42) to (x+204, y+12).
- **Tripler (33)**: moves in 5-tick steps of 10 px (|dx| >= 170) down to 2 px, hovers when |dx| < 7 and fires six pods one after another, each on a fixed animation frame (frame 0 for the 1st/2nd/3rd, frame 6 for the 4th/5th/6th; cooldown 12 ticks, 52 after the third), as type-17 bullets (speed 360) dropped straight down 20+35 px with per-pod offsets (y,x): (-55,-80) (83,-23) (-30,96) (9,-109) (66,69) (-72,41); then leaves. Their launch strength is 0, but the impact event (type 17, `0x40f590` -> `0x40fd30`) runs 34 sub-blasts: for the first 34 pairs of the ellipse table built at `0x412520` (`projectile::TRIPLER_RING`, radii about 64 x 41 px) with a random -5..4 px jitter it calls `0x42f2c0(x, y, 100, owner, 17)`, i.e. **34 x 100 area damage per pod, 204 blasts per strafe**; every ring point also creates a `blast_small` sprite (`0x405240(x, y, 1)` at `0x40fdae`) and, on a free cell that is not no-marks, two smoke puffs (`0x40fe8e`, `0x40ff16`; jitter and offsets from the visual generator `0x437cd0`), which draws the big burning ring; the game does the same (`World::step_bullets`, test `a_tripler_pod_lands_with_a_ring_of_blasts`).
- **Meteors**: both sizes are launched with strength 500 (`0x4685c9` type 24, `0x468614` type 25); no size-dependent damage exists in the exe, only the sprite and the half damage to buildings.
- Sound 64 (`hercules-hvizd`) has no call site in the exe (checked with a scan of every `0x4075b0` / `0x4077e0` call).
- **Carryall (31)**: always flies in from the right; speed = d+1 for distance d < 120 (so it nearly stops over the target), altitude = d for d in 41..=199 (descends and climbs again), unloads once when d < 5: `[+0x88]` squad (0..9, global counter `[0x89cf50]`, incremented per launch and wrapped to 0 at 10; the new value is used) lands on the 4x2 cells starting at (cell_x-1, cell_y) where free (`aircraft::squad`: e.g. squad 0 = four kind 7 and four kind 10, squad 5 = eight kind 1, squad 17 = two walkers 41); verified with `opensow squad`. With game mode `[0x49a33c] == 1` the squad is fixed (7,7,7,7,10,10).
- **Asteroid (34)**: 61 ticks after launch, then every 25 ticks, 20 times: one meteor (type 24 or 25, 50/50, strength 500, speed 240) falling from (x2+300, y2-400) to a random point (x2, y2) within 150 px of the target.
- Manual / strings: the Air Navigator (building 104) fills an icon bar before a strike is available; fighters are used to clear Anti-Air turrets and destroy Rogon nuke-bombs.

## Anti-air against aircraft (`0x47b3c0`, `aircraft::anti_air_target`)
The anti-air tops (units 1, 2, 3, 42 and the turret's gun unit 110, `[unit+0x4c]`) search aircraft in `0x46c990` on the top's 30-tick poll (`0x479cb5..0x479cdf`, args: the unit's position `[unit+0x14/0x18]`, its kept target `&[unit+0x80]`, the top's target `&[top+0x7c]`, range `[top+0x18]`..`[top+0x1c]` cells, own side). The range is **50 px per range cell** (`imul .., 0x32`), so the 0-9 anti-air reaches 450 px (the port used 9.5 x 32 = 304 px before). Sides 0..2 except the own one are scanned in slot order; aircraft with `hp <= 0` and **fighters** (code 32) are skipped. The kept target stays while `min*50 < d < max*50`, `d` measured to `x + [+0x18]`; any other aircraft becomes a candidate and the nearest by plain `x` (ties: the later slot) is taken if `min*50 <= d <= max*50`; nothing clears both targets and `0x47ad30` looks for an enemy Advancer. `[+0x18]` is 0 except for the bomber (+50 flying right, -50 left, `0x468063..0x468070`). While aiming and firing the top does not search again: `0x479902..0x47996a` only checks that the aircraft is still in its side's list (a falling one counts) and aims at `(x + [+0x18], y - altitude)`, which is also where the rockets fly. Tests: `anti_air_*` in `aircraft.rs`, `anti_air_engages_aircraft_out_to_fifty_pixels_per_range_cell`.

## Bombs
A bomb is bullet type 18, strength 110 (`0x4684e1`), from `(x+dx, y-alt+dy)` to `(x+reach+dx, y+dy)`; it takes the flight direction as `[+0x268c]` and draws `bomba_l` frame `progress` or, flying right, the pointer 20 slots further on, which is `bomba_r` (`0x5e9d88 + 4 * (frame + 20 * dir)`; the game resolves sprite pointers by address for that reason). Its impact (`0x40fb80`) creates `bomba_ex`, two puffs on free ground and the generic area damage `0x42f2c0`: **110 to every ground unit within 32 px** (`trunc(sqrt)`, own units included, flyers such as the Advancer skipped by `[unit+0x4b]`, `0x42f30e`) and 110 to the building whose cell it hits (none to a Headquarters). The radius is the same 32 px for every bullet type.

## Bomb acceleration (`0x41146d`)
The bullet step function adds the steps a type-18 bomb took this tick to its own speed `[bullet+0x2664]` (`cmp eax, 0x12; add [esi+0x2664], edx`), starting from the constructor's 30 (`0x411f4b`): `v' = v + steps(v)` with `steps ~ v / 60` per tick, so `v(t) ~ 30 e^(t/60)` and the 200 px fall takes about 120 ticks (2 s) instead of the 400 ticks of a constant 30 steps per second. No other type does this. `Bullet::speed`, test `a_bomb_falls_faster_and_faster`.

## Carryall squads are not random
The squad is the global counter `[0x89cf50]` (`0x468116..0x468127`: +1, wrapped to 0 at 10, stored as the carryall's `[+0x88]`), the only writes to it are that launch and the savegame loader (`0x42b150`; `0x42a615` saves it). It starts at 0 and is **never reset between missions**, so the first carryall of a mission drops whatever squad follows the carryalls launched before (scripted ones included); mission 1 ignores it (fixed 7,7,7,7,10,10 = six tier-1 units, `0x468970`). The squads in the cycle 1..9, 0 are four tier-2 units (1, 2), eight tier-1 (3, 5, 0 is four 7 + four 10), six (6, 7, 8, 9) and so on (`aircraft::squad`); there is no random draw in the launch or the unload (the `rand(30)` at `0x467f0f` only makes the aircraft handle). The game keeps the counter over missions (`Game::carry_counter`, `Play::new`).

## Launch lock (3 s)
`0x467ee0` for the local player (`side == [0x575730]`, every launch including the mission scripts') stores 180 in `[player+0x40]` (`0x467efb..0x467f03`). The HUD tick `0x454da0` counts it down by the frame's ticks (`0x454da9`, floor 0), and in the same tick sets the state of every air pill widget (`+0x3c`) to 2 while it is above 0 (`0x454e4e`: `cmovle eax, 0`, else 2). The widget draw `0x4560d0` switches on that state: 0 plain (`0x45edf0`), 1 additive, **2 half-additive `0x45c220`** (`0x4561d0` for the resting frame, `0x456140` for the hovered one), so a locked pill is drawn dimmed, like a locked slot icon; the hover glow (`+0x34`, additive) still comes on top. The hover updater `0x456350` runs for every pill regardless of the state (it sets `[+0x40]` = 0 / 1 and plays the hover sounds), but the hit test `0x454cf0..0x454d0a` only reports a pill as the hovered / clicked item when `[+0x3c] == 0`: for 180 ticks (3 s) there is no info box and a click does nothing (it falls through to the map). `World::strike_lock`, `Layout::pills_locked` (dimmed draw, no info box, clicks pass to the map); the B M C Y F keys (not in the exe: the key handler `0x415ba0` has no air-strike key, docs/hotkeys.md) respect it too.

## Strike click handler (local player, `0x416d00..0x417290`)
Icon/hotkey cases set `[ptr+0x1e4]` = force code (0x1e..0x22) and `[ptr+0x1e8]` = 1 (armed); `0x416f80` then shows pointer 10. The left-button release (`0x417028`) tests the air layer `0x60ac38` at the map pixel: `0xfffffd` (blocked for aircraft) launches nothing and stays armed (pointer 1); otherwise `[+0x1e8]` = 0, with remote players (`0x41f020`) the order is sent as a packet, else `0x467ee0` runs directly followed by `0x455520(force)` (stock - 1) and, for the local player, the `airforce-dest` ping (effect `0x44`) and the **`cross_as` marker** at the clicked point (`0x4171d5`: `AnimSprite(x, y, period 3, array 0x5e5fb0, frames 0..20)`, unsnapped, local player only; `World::launch_strike`). While armed the pointer is type 10 (`cursor.ps6`, docs/cursor.md; headless `--arm-strike CODE --mouse-px X,Y`). `0x41f387/0x41f3df/0x41f44e` are mission-script launches (fighters/carryalls at fixed positions, squad `[+0x88]`), `0x42ed6e..0x42f19b`/`0x42fa02` the AI/reinforcement launches, `0x468896` the carryall unload.

**Differs from the exe:** the exe passes `[ptr+0x40/0x44]` as the ping's position (`0x417221`); OpenSOW plays it as a non-positional priority sound (`launch_strike`, `player_sound(68)`).

## Tick scheduling of `0x467b50` (decoded)
Called once per aircraft per frame from the per-player loop `0x42dd19` (a dead one, return 0, is removed by `0x43a760`). The frame routine `0x42e890/0x42e9d0` emits k >= 0 ticks per frame (`[0x552494]`), stores the in-second index of each (`[0x517b10 + j*4]`, starting at the old `[0x5ddfc4]`) and then moves the clock: previous clock `[0x70ef8c]` (seconds) / `[0x70ef90]` (tick) = old `[0x70ef94]` / `[0x70ef98]`, current clock = previous + k. `0x467b50` runs **n = (sec_now - sec_prev) * 60 - tick_prev + tick_now = k** single-tick updates in a loop (`0x467bb0..0x467eb1`), the in-second index `ebx` starting at the previous clock's tick and wrapping at 60 (`0x467e86`); so it is exactly one update per game tick with the same tick index the other per-tick code uses. A catch-up flag `[+0xa8]` (set by `0x43a2c9`, a network path) replaces the previous clock with the aircraft's own saved `[+0xa0]/[+0xa4]`, so an aircraft that was missed runs the ticks since then at once; `0x467ee0` clears it. Per tick order: dead -> `0x467850` (false: removal); per-type case; bounds check (x < -400, or x > map width * 32 + 400 for the right-hand layers, returns removal). OpenSOW: one `Aircraft::step` per game tick, no catch-up flag (no network play).

**Differs from the exe:** OpenSOW's `step()` increments the tick first and all consumers use `tick % 60`, a constant shift of one against the exe's in-second index; it cannot change any per-second sum.

## Death sequence (`0x467850`, `Aircraft::die`)
Called every tick while `hp <= 0` (before the per-type case, which keeps flying a falling aircraft); returns 0 = removed. `[+0x3c]` (cloud timer, 0 at launch) counts down: at 0 it restarts at `30 + rand(5)`, plays sound 23 (`0x4077e0`) at (x, y - altitude) and spawns one `e_air2` sprite (52 frames, 2 ticks each, draw mode 6 or 7 by `[0x49a33a]`, `0x4054a0`): with `rand(4) == 0` at +-rand(50) vertically / +-rand(100) horizontally, else +-rand(30) / +-rand(60) (offset = `(rand(2)*2-1) * rand(n)`, the vertical one first). `[+0x38]` (180; fighter/asteroid 0) counts down too; at 0: three more sounds, four slow `e_air2` sprites (4 ticks per frame, wide spread), the player's kill statistic (`0x467b12`), removal. All draws use the lockstep generator `0x437b10`. An aircraft that merely leaves the map shows no effect. Unit test `a_falling_bomber_explodes_every_30_ticks...`; not emulation-verified.

## The computer's strike target (`0x435ad0`, `aircraft::ai_strike_target`)
Caller `0x42f930..0x42fa2b` (per tick of the frame): the timer `[0x70f2c8]` restarts at 600, `rand(3)` picks code 30 / 32 / 33, `0x454a60` must report stock, `0x435ad0(&x, &y, player)` must return 1, then `0x467ee0`, the packet `0x43a270` and `0x455520`. The pick: (1) any idle pod (kind 46) of another side, i.e. its position equals its destination, is the target; (2) else a density map `[0x71b7d8]` of 24 x 20 blocks of 128 px (`index = bx + 24*by`) from every unit but the Advancer: own units subtract 3 (block), 2 (left/right), 1 (above/below) for indices 0..=480; other sides' units add `3w/2w/w` with `w` = 2 for an idle walker (41..45), else 1, **only for indices 0..=256** (the lower half of a big map never attracts a strike; no column check, so a neighbour of column 23/0 wraps to the next/previous row); the scan keeps the first maximum as a signed byte and the strike goes to the block centre (`x*128+64`) when it is at least 9. Unit tests; not emulation-verified. OpenSOW: same in campaign missions (`step_ai`, `World::strike_target`).

**Differs from the exe:** skirmishes (not in the exe) on Hard and Brutal spend the stock with `sim/air_ai.rs` instead of the 600-tick pick, the strike period depends on the difficulty (`Difficulty::handicap`, Normal 600), and the computer calls in carryalls (`sim/airlift_ai.rs`); docs/skirmish.md.

- Carryall sound: `carryall-land` (62, positional at the aircraft, `0x467592`) plays twice per visit: on approach when closer than 190 px (`[+0x8c]`) and again when it climbs above 45 px while leaving (`[+0x8d]`, `0x467570`); the unload itself is silent. Heard only within ~667 px of the screen centre (docs/audio.md volume formula), so a carryall far from the camera is silent.

## Mission-script launches
All of them call `0x467ee0` for the local player and then overwrite `[+0x88]` (the squad); the global squad counter still advances. Countdown rewards (`0x42ec3a..0x42f1fa`): mission 7 squad 11, mission 12 squad 16 and **six** asteroids plus three bombers (`0x453e80` called with 0x22 six times, 0x1e three times), mission 17 squad 10. Level loader `0x41f140` on a fresh start (empty save name, not network): mission 1 two carryalls with squad 12 at (0x620, 0x6e0) and (0x6e0, 0x720) whose start x is moved by -200 / +200, mission 4 one with squad 17 (two walkers) at (0x360, 0xe0), x -300 (cases `0x41f3a2`, `0x41f34a`); in mission 1 the unload ignores the squad (mode-1 squad). `0x421d46` is the network packet receiver and `0x43d8f4` the info-box stat code, not scripts. Ported in `World::reinforcements`, `start_airlift`; test `mission_scripts_launch_carryalls_with_their_own_squads`.

## Not decoded
Nothing open in the air forces. Recharge times are the air base's production (`4000 / (30 + 12 * windtrap levels)` s, docs/production.md); the start stock is header words `5+3g` / `6+3g` (player / enemy) above. The game draws the aircraft with their sprites (`Play::draw_aircraft*`, body `0x467240`, shadow `0x4673a0`): bomber `bomber_l/r[frame + 10*dir]`, carryall `carryAll[frame]` (frame 0..4 from the distance to the target: approaching < 190/175/160/145 px, leaving < 190/180/170/160), tripler `tripler[frame]`, fighter `fighter[0]`, all plain at (x+[+0x7c], y+[+0x80]-altitude); the shadow is the same sprite through the shade blit `0x460f60` on the ground point, drawn in a pass before the units; bodies are drawn from the smallest y down (`0x4202f0`). A carryall also draws `carryAll_rear[0..3]` additive (exhaust, 3 steps per frame, unless x is in target-2..target+15) after the body and, in frame 4 and more than 1 px from the target x, `carryAll_down[0..1]` additive (5 steps per frame, 3 px up) before it. The standalone viewer does not draw aircraft.
The viewer shows the stock in the window title. The air pills' hover glow is `ikonyaura` frame 2 (`0x454014` stores `[0x5e7178]` in the pill's `+0x34`; the tabs use frame 1, the factory slots frame 0). Headless: `--strike CODE,CX,CY [--strike-god]` launches a force, `--mouse-px X,Y` hovers the HUD.

## Header words (status)
Words 2-3 are the start **credits** of player and enemy, words 37-38 the start research points, the other words the exe reads are listed in docs/ai.md (all others are ignored). Air-base slots (kinds 30-34, target 4000) add one force to the stock when full; the strike click calls `0x467ee0` and `0x455520` removes it from the stock; the AI launches a bomber, fighter or tripler every 600 ticks if it has one.

**Differs from the exe:** between the top's 30-tick polls the exe keeps the last aircraft without a range check, so a turret finished its volley at a bomber that had already left the range. `World::anti_air_aircraft` now drops a kept aircraft that is `>= max * 50` px away. Test `the_anti_air_turret_stops_shooting_when_the_bomber_flies_out_of_range`.
