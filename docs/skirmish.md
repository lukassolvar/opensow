# Skirmish (not in the original game)

Main menu row SKIRMISH (between MULTIPLAYER and OPTIONS) opens the port's own setup screen with the second side played by the computer. Code: `skirmish_ui.rs` (`SkirmishMenu`, the screen), `skirmish.rs` (turning its choices into a level), `Play::new_skirmish`, `Game::start_skirmish`. The multiplayer lobby (`multiplayer.rs`) is only the network one now; the skirmish shares its `Setup` (values, arrows, defaults) and `display_names`.

## The setup screen
A full-window page, not the original's 640x480 lobby: drawn at the window's own resolution in the main menu's face (every language), from the install's art only - the `NetworkBackground` picture (dimmed) behind everything, the map's ground for the preview, the aircraft as the game flies them (`specials\\fighter`, `bomber_r`, `tripler`, `carryAll`, `meteor_medium`, first frame) and the turrets as they stand on the map (finished base `builds\\blue\\sentry0N` + blue gun top facing south-east, `buildings::turret_art`), both enlarged through the 2x Lanczos copies, and the HUD's gold bars and light bulb (`extras\\ikony` frames 18 / 19) cut off their blue tile (`hud_icon`: pixels matching the colour most same-sized frames share, reachable from the frame's edge, are the tile; the bulb's glass stays) for credits and research (the info box's folders `1` and `7` are wireframes, `7` shows walkers). The air-force and turret cards show no names (the pictures say it); the computer's income / production sit on two lines beside its level, the language file's CREDITS / RESEARCH (275 / 276); panels, arrows, toggles and stars are drawn flat (the original arrow frames carry their old screen's background).
- **Layouts**: design 1920x1080 with three columns (map list | map picture and facts | players, starting resources, air forces, turrets) for windows from 1200x720; design 1024x768 with two columns below that, where the list becomes a map selector (arrows, wheel, Up / Down keys) above the picture. Both are scaled to fit and centred (`Geo`, `T`); controls and drawing share the same rectangles, tested at 640x480 .. 2560x1080 (`skirmish_ui::tests`).
- **Top bar**: title, subtitle, and a badge with the selected map's star. **Bottom bar**: BACK (Esc), the star rule note, Reset defaults, START (Enter).
- **Maps**: every `*.net` (base game first, Warmonger's after): Warmonger's maps continue the base game's numbering in title case (`09-The First Step` .. `14-Ground Zero`, `display_names`; file names unchanged). The list scrolls with the wheel when it does not fit and keeps the chosen row in view; a star marks maps already won.
- **Map picture**: the ground (`.srf`, reduced once to 720 px, `scene::thumbnail`) with the script's buildings and turrets (blue / green / grey by owner), a ring at each headquarters (the start positions) and the Advancers; facts below: size in cells, buildings (neutral ones), gold mines, the mission whose ground it is.
- **Players**: YOU and COMPUTER with their side colours; Swap sides chooses the side the human starts on; the computer's level steps Easy / Normal / Hard / Brutal (wrapping) with its income / production numbers.
- **Both sides are editable**: credits (step 500, max 19000), research (step 100, max 2000), the five air forces (cards with their pictures, a stepper per side) and the five turret tiers (cards with a toggle per side). Defaults come from the map: `Setup::from_level`, turret tiers from the map's own header bytes (`Setup::skirmish_default`; First Spark offers tiers 1-2 only).
- Sounds: 30 hover, 33 arrows / toggles / map selector, 39 buttons, 38 START.

## Stars
A small star at the right end of a map's row means the player has beaten the computer on it **with the map's default setup**: `SkirmishMenu::is_default` compares the screen's setup (both sides' credits, research, air forces and turret plates) with `Setup::skirmish_default` of the selected map; changing any of them - even back and forth to the same value is fine, only the final state counts - makes the match ineligible. Which side the human plays, the difficulty and the starting side do not matter. The star's colour is the hardest level won that way (`stars.rs`): bronze Easy, silver Normal, gold Hard, ruby Brutal; a harder win upgrades it, an easier one never lowers it. It is recorded when the victory banner appears (`Game::update`, `SkirmishRun::default_setup`) and kept in `skirmish-stars.txt` in the per-user data folder (`map=level` lines, key = the `.net` file name in lower case; tests never write it). The top badge says "Star: silver (Normal)" for the selected map and the bottom bar "Default setup: a win earns a star" / "Setup changed: no star". `--stars easy,normal,...` puts demo stars on the first maps for headless screenshots.

## Terrain of a `.net` map
A `.net` file is only a level script (header, buildings, units). It has no `.map` / `.srf` / `.til` / `.tmi`: the ground is the campaign mission **`M<level id>`** (header word 1) of the edition that owns the `.net`. Checked on all 14 shipped maps (`tests/real_data.rs::net_maps_are_played_on_the_terrain_of_their_level_id_mission`): every object lies inside that map and off its blocked cells (First Spark id 1 -> M01, Ice Blizzard 9 -> M09, Gold Rush 12 -> M12, Cross of Fire 17 -> M17, Warmonger's maps use Warmonger's M files). `GameDir::net_terrain` returns the right edition and name; `Scene::load_with` takes the terrain from the mission and the objects from the script.

## Map balance tweaks
Both sides of every shipped map start with the same resources, buildings and units; what differs is where things stand. Advancers fly straight and respawn at the HQ, so the main yardstick is each neutral building's flight distance from the two HQs (ground distance second). Where one side is clearly favoured, a skirmish edits the script at load time: the edits live in `data/skirmish-maps.txt` (moves, removals, additions, owner changes; made with the internal map editor, docs/map-editor.md), compiled in as `skirmish::TWEAKS` and applied by `skirmish::load_map` for the setup screen's picture and defaults, START and RESTART; the network lobby and the `.net` files are untouched:
- **06-Meteor Swarm**: nobody owns a gold mine at the start and blue's nearest neutral mine (4,16) is 14 cells by air from its HQ, green's 62. Blue's lies behind the ridge below its base (116 cells on foot from its HQ, 15.6 from its nearest turret), and green's side has no free spot like that within 24 cells, so the contested mine (27,62) (59 / 62 cells) moves behind the ridge above green's heavy factory to (75,47): 29 cells by air from green's HQ, 176 on foot (134 from blue's), 11 from green's nearest turret. Inside green's base (85,60) it would have been too safe: neutral, but under five tier-5 turrets.
- **08-Island of Fate** (no starting fighters: `stock fighter 0`, the 12 each side held were never used by the original routine and would be a free 12 strikes for the Hard/Brutal computer; decluttered too: the medium factories (6,4), (33,3), (16,18) and the edge windtraps (2,29), (91,41) are gone, 13 neutrals left, Advancer reach blue 23.6 / green 23.5): the only reachable research lab (47,3) is green's (36 cells by air from its HQ, 75 from blue's; the other lab sits on the bottom-right plateau that no ground unit reaches, 70 / 82 cells), and so are the nearer gold mines (26 and 33 cells vs blue's 33 and 55). Two plateau buildings go to blue's side, each a mirror of green's: the lab (85,73) to (38,47) (38 cells by air, 139 on foot for green, outside blue's turrets; green's lab: 36, 116 for blue) and the mine (91,75) to (24,47) (26 cells by air, 129 on foot for green, 15 from blue's nearest turret; green's mine (61,18): 26, 134, 17.7). The plateau keeps its mine (79,73), contested at 76 / 70 cells.

Tests: `skirmish::tests::the_tweaks_file_parses_and_leaves_maps_without_a_section_alone`, `every_tweak_names_an_object_and_puts_it_on_free_buildable_ground_of_the_real_map` (every edit names an object of the shipped file; every moved or added object stands on free buildable cells of the real map off the other footprints; both sides keep a headquarters). Stars won on a map before its tweak stay.

## Start health
Every skirmish map starts its owned buildings and turrets at full hit points and its neutral ones at half (`skirmish::even_start_health`, applied by `skirmish::rebalance` after the map tweaks). Object word 18 is the start percentage; the exe uses it for owned objects only (`Object::start_hp`, `0x43a003`; neutral ones always start at half), and the shipped `.net` files store owned ones anywhere from 69 % to 104 % (above 100 is capped), unevenly between the sides: Ground Zero (most turrets of both sides 69-97 %, both HQs), Great Crush (both HQs 96 / 97 %, turrets 81-90 %), The First Step (turrets 70-97 %, both HQs 99 %), Burn Them All (blue's three mines, green's heavy factory and tier-5 turret), and single 96-99 % objects on Ice Blizzard, Nuclear Devastation, Cross of Fire, Island of Fate, Goldmine Valley and Air Attacks. First Spark, Gold Rush, Meteor Swarm and Winter War had none. Tests: `skirmish::tests::owned_objects_start_full_and_neutral_ones_half`, `every_skirmish_map_starts_owned_objects_full_and_neutral_ones_half` (all maps of the install).

## Starting the match
`skirmish::prepare(map script, setup, human side)`:
1. `Setup::apply` writes both sides' credits / research / air forces / turret switches into the header words the sim reads (inverse of `from_level`).
2. With the human on green, `swap_sides` exchanges every object owner (0 <-> 1, neutral stays), unit-marker player, and the header pairs (credits 2/3, Advancer rates 31/32, no-respawn flags 34/35, research 37/38, air forces 5+3g / 6+3g, turret bytes 84.. / 94..), so the human is always player 0 and the computer player 1.
Then the ordinary `World::from_level` / `Play` run with `ai = true` (the computer opponent of docs/ai.md). RESTART from the pause menu replays the same choices; the main menu forgets them.

## Difficulty
The original has no difficulty setting and its AI is an exe-verified port (docs/ai.md), so the levels never change what the computer decides, only how rich and fast it is and how often it re-decides (`sim::Difficulty`, `Handicap`; they apply to side 1 only, and only while `World::ai` is on, so the campaign and the human are never touched):

| level | income (gold, research) | production speed | idle units / Advancers re-ordered | air strike every |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 60 % | 70 % | every 120 ticks (2 s) | 1200 ticks |
| **Normal** | 100 % | 100 % | every tick (the exe) | 600 ticks (the exe) |
| Hard | 150 % | 130 % | every tick | 420 ticks |
| Brutal | 250 % | 180 % | every tick | 240 ticks |

Income is the per-second yield scaled in hundredths with the remainder carried (`World::income_rest`); production scales the rate row (`30 + 12 * windtrap levels`) before `steps_at`. The computer's start resources are the lobby's own numbers (no extra bonus: set them in its column). Tests: `sim::tests::difficulty_scales_the_computers_income_only_and_levels_are_ordered`, `an_easy_computer_re_orders_idle_units_only_every_two_seconds`. Headless: `--difficulty easy|normal|hard|brutal`.

## Limits
- The human is always drawn in player 0's colour (blue), also when starting on the green side; the computer is green.
- Win/lose is the sim's ordinary "destroy everything" rule; no results screen options of the campaign.
- Saving a skirmish is not supported (a slot stores a level name; `01-First Spark` is no mission).

## Headless
`opensow-game --skirmish "03-Gold" [--side green] [--difficulty hard] [--credits BLUE,GREEN] [--research BLUE,GREEN] --ticks N --screenshot out.png [--size WxH] [--center CX,CY] --report` starts a match from a (prefix of a) map name. `--screen skirmish [--map N] [--guest] [--changed] [--hover N] [--stars ...] [--difficulty L] --screenshot out.png [--size WxH]` renders the setup screen (`--guest` swaps sides, `--changed` raises green's credits once, `--hover` 0-4 an air-force card, 5-9 a turret card). Always pass `--screenshot`; without it the game opens a real window.

## Translations
The skirmish texts are not in the game's language files, so `crates/opensow-game/src/tr.rs` holds this port's own translations (English, Slovak, Czech, Chinese simplified / traditional): the main menu row and every text of the setup screen (headings, players, buttons, map facts, star lines, setup notes, level names, the income line); air-force, turret and resource names come from the language files. Other languages read English. The error message of a failed start stays English (it is drawn with the bitmap font).

## Main menu font
Every language draws the main menu rows as GDI text from its `lang*.txt` (strings 0..5, plus the skirmish row from `tr.rs`), English included; the English art buttons are only a fallback when the language file is missing (`Assets::main_labels`).

## Turrets like a player (skirmish only, not in the exe)
In a skirmish (`World::skirmish`, set by `Play::new_skirmish`) the computer does not run the exe's turret routine (`0x431120`, docs/ai.md item 7, which keeps buying the dearest tier with no limit); `World::ai_defend` feeds `sim/defence.rs` instead. Campaign missions are untouched.
- **Timing:** it looks every 60 ticks while an own building is under pressure, every 240 otherwise (twice as long on Easy), and builds at most one gun per look.
- **Situation per building:** enemy pressure = enemy ground units within 9 cells of it (or after it within 18): 1 per tank, 2.5 per walker, 0.3 per anti-air unit; cover = ground firepower of the own guns that reach it (tier points 2 / 0 / 3 / 4 / 4 for tiers 1-5); worth (HQ 10, heavy factory 8, medium and walker factory and gold mine 7, light factory, air base and lab 5, windtrap 4); exposure 0..1 (nearest to the enemy HQ = 1).
- **Want (purely reactive, no purse, no peace time):** a building with enemy units pressing on it (within 9 cells of its edge, or heading for it within 18) wants 1.5 tier points of firepower per unit of attacking weight (tank 1, walker 2.5), at most 16 and never more than 5 guns; without attackers nothing. Anti-air (tier 2), where an enemy Advancer works on or hovers within 6 cells of a building: two turrets cover it while the Advancer works on the building itself, three once it starts on the turrets that cover it (`Gun::attacked`), and four (killed before it reaches the building) when the credits are 1200+ and no building needs ground guns; none without an Advancer.
- **Buy:** the most expensive allowed gun the credits pay for (artillery, tier 5, minimum range 2, only behind a direct gun); when nothing is affordable it waits and saves. Up to four guns per look, each decision seeing the guns just bought; the planner looks every second while a building is attacked.
- **Place:** every free cell within 4 of the building (the normal placement test) is scored: facing the danger (the attackers' centre, else the enemy HQ) for direct guns, behind the building for artillery; one cell clear of the walls; -2.5 next to another own gun, -0.8 two cells away; +0.6 (anti-air +1) for every other building it also reaches; factory exits, the cells after them and rally points (and their neighbours) stay free.

Tests: `sim::defence::tests` (pure) and `in_a_skirmish_one_attacker_gets_one_gun_and_a_wave_several`, `the_skirmish_planner_waits_between_looks`. Headless: on 03-Gold with default resources the computer has 2 guns after 2 minutes and 12 (one anti-air) after 10, spread over its captured buildings.

## The Advancer like a player (skirmish only, not in the exe)
The exe's pick (`0x42fae0`, docs/ai.md rule 5) scores every building once, gives foreign anti-air turrets a **bonus** and keeps the target until it is done; a shot-down Advancer respawns at the HQ (`0x42e38d`) and flies to the same target again, so it loops. In a skirmish `World::ai_advancers` feeds `sim/advancer_ai.rs` instead (campaign missions unchanged), every 30 ticks (90 on Easy):
- **Jobs:** capture foreign and neutral buildings and turrets (not satellite-protected ones), upgrade own buildings with a locked slot, repair own buildings below 75% hp (not while they heal in after a capture). Worth: HQ 10, gold mine 9, heavy/walker factory 8, medium factory 7, lab 6, light factory and air base 5, windtrap and A.A. turret 4, other turrets 3.
- **Priorities:** a capture is worth 3x the building (4.2x when taken from the enemy; turrets 0.7x, they are only removed) times the nearness `1 / (1 + d/16)`, `d` = cells from the Advancer or the own HQ, whichever is nearer; an upgrade 1.5x the building, every level alike; **an Advancer hovering over an own building that can still be upgraded stays and finishes every level first** (also right after capturing one: upgrades may start while it heals in); a repair the worth times the missing hp share, and **repairs come last**: any feasible capture or upgrade is taken first, and one turning up while it flies to a repair takes over. On 03-Gold it upgrades its two gold mines to their last level, takes the neutral mine next to its base, upgrades its factory and mines, then expands to the other mines, the lab and a factory (11 buildings after 9 minutes).
- **Time and risk:** flight at one cell per 64 ticks plus beam work (one shot per 10 ticks: `2*rate` hp for destroy and repair, `rate` points for upgrades); expected damage = fire along the straight route + fire at the work cell x work time (A.A. turret 0.45 hp/tick within 9 cells, anti-air units 0.3 (walker 42: 0.5) and enemy Advancers 0.3 within their range). A job that would cost more than 80% of the remaining hp is not started; score = value per minute of flight + work (+20 s), times `1 - risk share`. So a lone A.A. turret can be taken out, two together are left alone.
- **Watching the job:** done or gone -> next job; lost 45% of max hp on it under fire -> break off and fly to the nearest own building out of enemy reach; no step closer for 480 ticks while not working -> unreachable; while flying it switches only to a job scoring twice as much, never during the beam work. Two Advancers never take the same job.
- **Memory** (`World::adv_mind`, saved): a target where the Advancer was shot down, driven off or stuck is avoided for 3600 ticks, doubled per repeat up to 8x, unless the fire there has halved since (a failure without known fire holds for the whole time).
- **Brute force:** when no job is safe enough, it does not wait. Beam damage and the capture tally stay when the Advancer is shot down, and the HQ builds a new one for free, so it pushes the job where one life gets the most done (`advancer_ai::forced_job`): worth (captures times their nearness) x the share of the work it can finish before its hp run out after the flight there (all of it without fire there), per minute, divided by `1 + 0.5 x` recent failures there, so equal targets take turns. A forced job is not broken off under fire; it gives way to any safe job that turns up while flying. Left out: targets it cannot even reach alive, satellite-protected ones and ones remembered as unreachable (no route).
- **Idle:** only when not even a forced job exists does it wait at the nearest safe own building.

Tests: `sim::advancer_ai::tests` (pure) and `the_skirmish_advancer_takes_the_open_building_first_then_pushes_the_guarded_one`, `a_skirmish_advancer_shot_down_does_not_return_to_the_same_target`.

## Carryalls (skirmish only, not in the exe)
The original computer never launches a carryall (its strike pick `rand(3)` chooses bomber, fighter or tripler only, docs/ai.md item 6), so a stock of carryalls (lobby, or its air bases) just piled up. In a skirmish `World::ai_airlift` asks `sim/airlift_ai.rs` once per second (campaign missions unchanged; test `the_campaign_computer_never_calls_a_carryall`). A carryall flies in from the right edge along its target's row, hovers ~200 ticks and drops the next squad of the global cycle (`aircraft::squad`) on the 4x2 block `(x-1..x+2, y..y+1)`, filled column by column from the left.

Like the player, the computer **does not know which squad comes**; it plans with the average load of the ten-squad cycle (`airlift_ai::expected_strength`, unit strength: walkers 2.5, anti-air units 0.3, others 1) and decides what the units do only when they have landed.

**1. Calling a carryall** (`decide`):
- **Reinforce** an own building (not a turret) with enemy ground pressure >= 2 that beats its defence (own units within 9 cells + own turrets' power / 2), aiming 40% of the way from the building to the attackers. Value = worth x the missing strength (capped by the average load).
- **The enemy Advancer** working on or within 6 cells of an own building, aiming two cells beside it (the load may hold anti-air units). Value = the building's worth.
- **Raid** an enemy or neutral building (not satellite-protected; of the turrets only the A.A. turret, which keeps the sky closed) that the average load outguns (`strength >= 1.5 x defence + 2`), aiming 3 cells west of it. Value = worth (x1.4 enemy) x strength / (defence + 2). Raids keep one carryall back: they need two in stock or a quiet minute (3600 ticks) since the last launch.
- **Where it lands:** the cell nearest to the aim point (within 10 cells) that **no anti-air covers** (A.A. turrets within 9 cells, anti-air units within their range), whose two left landing columns are free plus at least two more cells of the block, and whose row flight (16 ticks per cell, 0.45 / 0.3 hp per tick) stays below 70% of the carryall's 360 hp. A spot that overlaps the block of a carryall of the side still in the air is skipped, so **carryalls called in sequence land side by side**. No such spot: no launch.
- Only calls worth 4 or more fly. `World::airlifts` keeps the drops in flight (forgotten when the carryall is shot down).
- **Per difficulty** (`airlift_ai::Tuning`, from `World::difficulty`):

  | | launches apart | in the air at once | raids | raids on the player's buildings | last carryall raids after a quiet minute |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Easy | 1800 ticks | 1 | none (defence and the enemy Advancer only) | - | no |
  | Normal | 1800 ticks | 1 | yes | from minute 5, never within 16 cells of the player's HQ | no |
  | Hard | 900 ticks | 2 | yes | from minute 2, not within 8 cells of the HQ | yes |
  | Brutal | 600 ticks | 3 | yes | at any time, anywhere | yes |

  Landed units follow the same raid rules (on Normal they do not go for a building by the player's HQ).
- **No carryalls at the start:** `Setup::skirmish_default` zeroes both sides' carryall stock whatever the map holds (Ice Blizzard had 3 each, which let the computer drop squads into the player's base in the first seconds); air bases still build them and the lobby can add some (then the game earns no star, as for any change). The one map that relied on them, **01-Goldmine Valley**, gives each side 12 tier-1 ground units instead, the same for both and no anti-air: six cannons (7) and two each of artillery (4), flamers (10) and specials (13) (`skirmish::start_units`). `World::add_start_units` stands them in two blocks of six (3x2, filled column by column so each column is a pair of one kind, like a carryall squad): the cannons, then the artillery, flamers and specials. Each block goes out into the open, away from the base: its middle 6 to 18 cells from the HQ's middle, every cell free (no blocked terrain, building, unit or factory exit) and not under a tree or rock top of the map's foreground layer (`.tmi`), no building or unit on the ring of cells around it, and reachable on foot from the HQ; of these the nearest by walking distance (so not a plateau behind a cliff). With no such spot the units go one by one to the nearest free cells of that range. On Goldmine Valley both sides' blocks stand in the open field next to their base.

**2. Using what came** (`assign`, in the unload): anti-air units go for the enemy Advancer if it is within 25 cells; the other units go for (a) the nearest attacker of the nearest own building within 14 cells that is losing a fight, else (b) the nearest foreign building within 14 cells they outgun (`1.5 x defence + 1 <= their strength`; shooting it down captures it), else (c) the nearest enemy unit within 10 cells, else the normal AI takes them over.

On 02-Ice Blizzard (no carryalls at the start, the air bases build them) on Normal the first carryall flies after ~4.5 minutes and 8 fly in 15 minutes, none near the player's HQ; on Brutal 14 fly in the same time, also into the player's base.

Tests: `sim::airlift_ai::tests` (pure: blind call, anti-air-free spot, follow-ups side by side, room to land, goals by unit type), `the_skirmish_computer_airlifts_a_squad_to_a_building_under_attack`, `the_campaign_computer_never_calls_a_carryall`.

## Anti-air units and Advancers waiting for a capture
- The computer's anti-air units chase an enemy Advancer only within 30 cells; otherwise they go to the enemy HQ with the other units (body shield).
- An Advancer whose building is in the destruction animation keeps its job and waits (`Adv::holding`), so it is still over the building when the capture completes and upgrades it at once instead of flying off.

## Advancer priorities on Easy and Normal
`Mind::tiered` (set from the difficulty; Hard and Brutal keep the gain/time weighing): 1. upgrades of own buildings at any distance, also one just captured (nearest first, one it hovers over before all); 2. neutral buildings within 20 cells of where the Advancer is now (nearest first); 3. the opponent's buildings and far neutral ones by the usual gain; 4. turrets; repairs last. Risk from anti-air still rules a job out.
Hard and Brutal use the same order (`Mind::bold` 1/2) with a middle ground: the opponent's gold mine and heavy/walker factories (Brutal: also lab, medium factory, any worth >= 6, and neutral captures out to 30 cells) join step 2, ranked there by worth over distance. Easy and Normal leave the opponent's buildings to step 3.

## Spectating computer vs computer (dev tool)
`opensow-game --skirmish "03-Gold" --spectate [--difficulty D] [--blue-difficulty D]` plays the computer for both sides (`World::spectate`; green uses `--difficulty`, blue `--blue-difficulty`, default the same). Windowed it is a normal game window with the free camera, the game-speed keys (and `--dev x4`); the human controls still exist but there is nothing to command. Blue's computer shares the code of green's: `step_ai(0)` runs with its own copy of the strategy/strike/defence timers (`step_ai_spectate`), income and production handicaps and the per-side difficulty apply to both (`World::difficulty_of`). The end shows "Blue/Green wins after M:SS" (no stars, no results). Headless it works with `--ticks N --report --screenshot`, which doubles as a balance check between difficulties.

## Air strikes on Hard and Brutal
`sim/air_ai.rs` (`World::ai_air`), skirmish only: the exe's routine (a random force every 600 ticks at a density peak in the upper map) left most of the stock unused (Island of Fate, Normal: 12 fighters each still in stock after 15000 ticks). On Hard and Brutal every second the stock is weighed: bomber (radius 4.5) and tripler (2.5) go where many enemy units and buildings stand together, anti-air in reach halves their value and three rule it out; the fighter (immune to anti-air) goes for anti-air first, then the biggest cluster; own units in the blast count against a spot; the bar is 5 points, 3 with 3+ of a force in stock, 2 with 6+; at most one launch per 6 s (Hard) / 3 s (Brutal). Same run on Brutal: all 12 fighters per side launched. Easy and Normal keep the exe's routine. `--report` prints the air stock and launches.
The computer's defence no longer counts the opponent's fighters when deciding on anti-air turrets (they are immune to anti-air): only bombers and triplers in stock call for it.

## Retaking lost buildings
The Advancer (`Mind::tiered`) retakes a building the opponent captured within 20 cells of its HQ before any other capture (tier 3.5), and does not repair turrets any more (they are replaced; two hurt turrets under fire kept it busy forever).
