OpenSOW 0.4.2 adds the original's Ctrl / Alt attack key, Warmonger's own cheat phrases and saves that keep your view bookmarks. If you already have 0.4.1, the launcher offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Hold Ctrl or Alt to attack, as in the original.** With either key held, a click on an enemy building orders a lasting attack that your units keep up until it falls; a plain click sends them to it once. The key also lets you attack your own units and buildings, and the attack pointer tilts while it is held.
- **Warmonger's own cheat phrases.** The Warmonger campaign now checks the phrases of its own original, including the one cheat only Warmonger has. A typed cheat now shows as a chat line in both campaigns, as in the original.
- **Saves keep your view bookmarks.** The numpad bookmarks and where Tab and H continue are saved and come back on load. Every mission starts with the original's nine default bookmarks, so each numpad key moves the view from the start.

## Known limitations

- No network play yet; skirmish is against the computer.
- Polish, Hungarian and Russian copies of the game run with English menus.
- Unit pathfinding still differs from the original in a few corner cases, so units can occasionally take a slightly different path.

Please [report what you find](https://github.com/lukassolvar/opensow/issues/new/choose). OpenSOW is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the original developers or publishers.
