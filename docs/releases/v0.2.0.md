The second beta of OpenSOW brings your own controls and a round of fixes from the first players' reports. If you already have 0.1.0, the launcher offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Controls menu.** A CONTROLS button on the options page opens three pages of hotkeys (camera, units, game). Every hotkey has two slots that take a key or an extra mouse button (middle, back, forward and others); a key bound elsewhere moves over, right click clears a slot, and DEFAULTS brings back the original keys.
- **Swap mouse buttons.** A new setting on the second options page and in the launcher's Extras tab puts orders (move, attack, the Advancer's repair, destroy and upgrade) on the right click. Selecting, scrolling, the HUD and the menus keep the original buttons. Off by default.

## Fixed

- **No grid lines when the picture is scaled.** At a scale that is not a whole number, such as 1024x768 on a 1920x1080 screen, some rows and columns were drawn twice and looked like a grid over the map. The picture is now scaled smoothly while game pixels stay sharp.
- **The mouse stays in the fullscreen game** with two displays instead of wandering onto the other screen.
- **Short-range walkers attack buildings and turrets** they stand next to, instead of walking on the spot.
- **Units hide behind map scenery** such as the towers in mission 4, as in the original.
- **Slovak and Czech texts:** typos in the game's own language files are corrected, and accented letters on the statistics screen no longer go missing.
- **Mission hints** are drawn with a black shadow like the original, no longer looking doubled.
- **Windows launcher:** choosing the game folder no longer opens an empty console window, the dialog opens in front, and folders with accented letters (such as `C:\Users\Matúš`) work.

## Known limitations

- No network play yet; skirmish is against the computer.
- Polish, Hungarian and Russian copies of the game run with English menus.
- A few details are not yet checked against the original: how a building looks while it is being destroyed, and some of Warmonger's mission-number rules.
- Unit pathfinding uses a simplified version of the original's grid, so units can occasionally take a slightly different path.

Please [report what you find](https://github.com/lukassolvar/opensow/issues/new/choose). OpenSOW is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the original developers or publishers.
