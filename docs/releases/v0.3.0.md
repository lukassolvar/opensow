OpenSOW 0.3.0 makes the launcher tell you what changed and when an update is waiting, and fixes two things players noticed in battle. If you already have 0.2.0, the launcher offers this update by itself.

## Fixed

- **Anti-air turrets play their launch sound** when they fire, as in the original; before, only the impact was heard.
- **Smooth flames when zoomed in.** Close up, flamethrower fire made the game stutter; its glow is now several times cheaper to draw.

## Launcher

- **Version and update badge.** The launcher's sidebar shows its version on every page. When a new release is out, a pulsing badge appears on the icon and the version line shows the new number; click it to get to the update.
- **What's new on the home page.** A card lists the changes of your version at a glance. When an update waits, it shows the new release's changes with an Install button. The notes come in English, Slovak, Czech and Chinese.
- **GitHub link.** The sidebar links to the project's page, where you can report problems and follow development.
- **A smoother launcher.** Pages fade and slide into place, buttons react to the pointer and the settings gear spins. The launcher still uses no processor time while nothing moves.
- **Tidier launcher pages:** the large icon and tagline on the home page and the subtitles on the game pages are gone, and the Warmonger entry carries the game's full name.

## Known limitations

- No network play yet; skirmish is against the computer.
- Polish, Hungarian and Russian copies of the game run with English menus.
- A few details are not yet checked against the original: how a building looks while it is being destroyed, and some of Warmonger's mission-number rules.
- Unit pathfinding uses a simplified version of the original's grid, so units can occasionally take a slightly different path.

Please [report what you find](https://github.com/lukassolvar/opensow/issues/new/choose). OpenSOW is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the original developers or publishers.
