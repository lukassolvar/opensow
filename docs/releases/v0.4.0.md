OpenSOW 0.4.0 changes the skirmish computer and its difficulty levels, brings unit movement closer to the original, plays each campaign's intro once and lets custom maps use longer convoy routes. If you already have 0.3.x, the launcher offers this update by itself.

## New

- **Skirmish computer uses the campaign's routines.** Its Advancer, turrets and anti-air units now follow the same logic as in the campaign: the Advancer no longer repairs its own HQ, and anti-air units go after your Advancer wherever it is. Carryall drops and the aimed air strikes on Hard and Brutal stay.
- **Skirmish difficulty rebalanced.** The computer's income and production are no longer multiplied. Easy keeps the normal economy and only reacts and strikes more slowly; Hard gives the computer one extra windtrap level and Brutal two.
- **Each campaign's intro plays once, right before its campaign.** The intro no longer plays at every start. Starting a new game plays the chosen campaign's intro before its map, and the first switch to Warmonger plays Warmonger's own intro. Each intro plays only once, also after a restart; skipping it counts.
- **Longer convoy routes in the map editor.** An escort route now takes up to 32 waypoints instead of 8. A route with a leg units cannot walk shows one "Invalid convoy path" warning and draws that leg in red, and the skirmish screen will not start such a map. OpenSOW 0.3 and older read only the first 8 waypoints of a longer route.

## Fixed

- **Units wait for each other as in the original.** A unit standing on the next cell now holds a walker back and makes it go around, and chasing units route around other units instead of pushing into them.
- **Advancers fly around other Advancers** instead of planning a route straight through them.
- **Destroyed buildings look right while they fall.** A dying building shows its destroyed frame instead of the damaged one, and a destroyed turret leaves its wrecked base behind while only the gun disappears.
- **Warmonger's own mission hints.** The Warmonger campaign shows its own hint texts in English, as its original does, including the fighter hint about enemy turrets.

## Known limitations

- No network play yet; skirmish is against the computer.
- Polish, Hungarian and Russian copies of the game run with English menus.
- Unit pathfinding still differs from the original in a few corner cases, so units can occasionally take a slightly different path.

Please [report what you find](https://github.com/lukassolvar/opensow/issues/new/choose). OpenSOW is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the original developers or publishers.
