OpenSOW 0.4.1 is a bug-fix release for unit movement, turrets and two campaign details. If you already have 0.4.0, the launcher offers this update by itself.

## Fixed

- **Units no longer get stuck behind a standing unit.** A unit that found another unit parked on its path could stand still for good; now it goes around, as in the original.
- **Large turrets aim from their gun.** Turrets 4 and 5, which take two cells, measured their reach from the top cell instead of the gun, so they could hit targets one row too far up and miss ones in range below. Their placement range in the build menu uses the gun too.
- **Mission 7's Advancer arrives with the reinforcements.** In State of War's Sudan and Warmonger's Territory 06 you started with an Advancer that the briefing only promises later; now it comes when the countdown runs out, as in the original.
- **"New turret available" only for turrets you can build.** The announcement no longer plays for turret types the mission does not allow.

## Known limitations

- No network play yet; skirmish is against the computer.
- Polish, Hungarian and Russian copies of the game run with English menus.
- Unit pathfinding still differs from the original in a few corner cases, so units can occasionally take a slightly different path.
