# Computer opponent, economy and tutorial state (decoded from the exe)

Code: `crates/opensow-game/src/sim/computer.rs` (`step_ai`, `ai_build_turret`), `sim/economy.rs` (`step_economy`, `tech_up`, `place_turret`), `sim/mission.rs` (win/lose, scripts, hints, HQ), `crates/opensow-formats/src/turretai.rs`, `crates/opensow-formats/src/production.rs`, `advancer.rs`.

## Who runs the AI
Per tick and per player (loop `0x42da61`): the local player reads input; every other player (not the neutral index 2) runs `0x42f490(player)` when there are no remote players (`[0x575720] == 0`), `[0x517ae9] == 0` and **`[0x70f2cc] == -1`**. `[0x70f2cc]` is the **tutorial step** (0..0x1b, written ~60 times by the tutorial script `0x432d6e..0x433b6f`), -1 everywhere else; so the AI plays every mission and skirmish except the M00 tutorial, and the tutorial has its own script. OpenSOW: `World::step_ai` (`sim/computer.rs`) for side 1 every tick (both sides when spectating), not in the tutorial. (`[0x49a33c]` is the mission/game number; `0x42e580` holds small scripts for missions 1, 3 and 8, `0x42ebd0` the reinforcement timers of missions 7, 12 and 17.)

## `0x42f490(player)` in order
1. **Tech-levels**: for every building and every active slot with `level < max level` the AI buys the next tier when research >= the slot's price (`0x40e730`).
2. Finds an enemy HQ `[ebp-0x10]` and an enemy Advancer, counts enemy turrets (`esi`).
3. **Strategy** `[player+0x70ba88]` (1..4) with timer `[+0x70ba8c]`: re-rolled when the timer ends (rand(4)+1, next in 900+rand(600) ticks) or immediately when (no enemy turrets and strategy 2), (more than 4 enemy turrets and strategy != 2) or strategy 0. Afterwards: no enemy turrets and strategy 2 -> 4; more than 4 turrets -> 2. **The first roll is the same in every run of a mission:** a fresh mission start (state 11, `0x4342d0` -> `0x422b00`) puts the lockstep table back to index 0 (`0x422b19`), builds the 200 debris curves from it (`0x422c13` -> `0x40f2c0`, 600 numbers), then loads the level (`0x41f140`): every unit draws `rand(20)` and `rand(900)` in its initialiser (`0x46bf31`, `0x46c111`, both unconditional), and every building is created healed in (`0x41df6f` passes 1, so `0x439e30` calls `0x40d860`: pending heal paid out, `[b+0x68]` stays 1). The clock reset `0x4289d0` leaves one tick for the first frame, whose per-player loop (`0x42da61..0x42ddfe`) runs, for player 0, 1, 2 in turn, input or AI (`0x42dc22`) and then that player's buildings (`0x40d9a0`, `0x40de60`: a turret whose `[b+0x68]` is still 1 creates its gun unit, two more draws), aircraft, units and bullets. So player 1's first strategy roll comes after player 0's turret guns and before its own; inside the AI the Advancer pick `0x42fae0` (called at `0x42f602`, before the strategy) draws `rand(3)` for every building of all three players for each idle Advancer. Weapon tops draw only when their 30-tick search finds a target (`0x479cf3`). M06 (19 units, 3 blue turrets, 42 buildings): 600 + 38 + 6 + 42 = **index 686**, `table[686] % 4 = 0` -> **strategy 1**, timer 900 + `table[687] % 600` = 1224: every idle enemy unit walks to the player's HQ at the start, never to the neutral research lab (static count, not emulated).
   OpenSOW: the same stream. `World::from_level_with_rng` installs the table before the level's units are built; `World::step` runs the exe's per-player turns (`sim/turns.rs`, "Update order" below); the Advancer pick (`ai_pick_buildings`) runs before the roll; weapon tops draw their reload jitter from the table (`TopAim::tick`, only on acquiring a target); aircraft draw from the table; a turret's gun draws `rand(20)` and `rand(900)` when created. `--play M06` rolls at index 686 (strategy 1, timer 1224); test `m06_rolls_the_exes_first_strategy_at_the_exes_table_position` (real data). Before, the port drew from the fallback generator during the load and seeded a private generator on every weapon tick, and M06 rolled strategy 4 (the two cannons went for the lab).
4. **Idle units** (no target `[unit+0x7c]`, top alive, not the Advancer, `[unit+0x4c]` = 0) are ordered to attack (`0x469fe0`, the unit then chases with a path replanned every 7 ticks, `0x46eb40`): target classes come from a bounded flood fill over the walkable map (`0x472a90`, 8000 cells, "Unit find way unreachable!!!"; ported as `wave::find_targets`, exact vs emulation of 300 random grids: a wave over the static grid `0x5eac38` that holds terrain, building handles and the ground units' handles; free cells are stamped and expanded x-1, x+1, y-1, y+1; an object cell is a leaf; every object whose owner differs from the player counts, **neutral buildings included**; the first unit / turret (building type 3 or 6) / other building met wins; the wave ends after a generation of more than 8000 cells or when the stamp passes base+8000): nearest enemy unit, nearest turret, nearest other building. Strategy 1 = HQ; 2 = turret, unit, building; 3 = unit, turret, building; 4 = building, turret, unit; fallback the HQ; missions 3 and 8 always unit then HQ (`0x42f81d..0x42f828` jump to `0x42f86e`; the Warmonger exe has the same check at `0x42eabd` on its mission global `0x491360`, so Warmonger's convoy mission M03 plays by it too (it has no M08), whatever the strategy: the pods are units, so the computer hunts the convoy; OpenSOW: `step_ai`, also for a custom map's escort (not in the exe), with the one-shot order of item 4 there too; test `in_the_escort_missions_the_computer_goes_for_the_pods_not_the_headquarters`; the port used to apply the strategy here too and sent the units at the HQ). Units with `[+0x4c] != 0` (anti-air) go for the enemy Advancer.
   **The order is one route, not a lasting target** (`0x42f882`: `0x469fe0(target, 0)`): `0x469fe0` stores the target in `[unit+0x7c]`, clears `[chassis+0x158]`, runs the chase check `0x46eb40` once (one route to the cell `range` steps before the target via `0x477630`; nothing when the unit is already within `range - 1`) and then, because its flag argument is 0 and the unit is not a pod, **clears `[unit+0x7c]` again** (`0x46a141..0x46a14d`). So the unit does not follow a moving target, its weapon has no order target (the priority scan `0x47b140` picks unit, turret, building in range), and the computer picks again only when the route has ended (`[chassis+0xe0] == 0`) with the strategy of that moment. A unit sent to a neutral building therefore shoots it only while it stands there and leaves at the next strategy that points elsewhere. The gate also needs an enemy HQ (`[ebp-0x10] != 0`, `0x42f7fb`), else no unit is ordered at all. OpenSOW (campaign): `step_ai` runs `chase_step` once and keeps no `chase`; test `a_campaign_computer_order_is_one_route_and_the_unit_picks_again_when_it_ends`.
   **Differs from the exe:** a unit counts as idle only without a chase target *and* without a route (`step_ai`); without an enemy HQ the port still orders units to the targets the wave found.
   **Differs from the exe:** skirmish (not in the exe) keeps the computer's order as a lasting chase (`Unit::chase`, replanned every 7 ticks), which its own squad logic relies on; against a custom map's escort it takes the exe's one-shot order.
5. **Advancer** (`0x42fae0`, only when idle): every building gets `rand(3)`; HQs -10000; own turrets and own buildings without free upgrade slot -10000; foreign satellite-protected buildings (`[b+0x114]`) -10000; foreign turrets +2 (anti-air turret 26 another +2); foreign buildings + their active slots; minus the distance; -2 for each already chosen target within 5 cells; the best building is destroyed (foreign), upgraded (own) or repaired (own turret, never chosen in practice).
   **Differs from the exe:** `step_ai` leaves out the -2 for already chosen targets within 5 cells, so several Advancers of one side can pick the same building.
6. **Air strikes** (never carryalls): every 600 ticks `[0x70f2c8]` it picks Bomber (30), Fighter (32) or Tripler (33) at random (`rand(3)`, `0x42f948`; never the asteroid), and if it has stock and `0x435ad0` finds a target position (`aircraft::ai_strike_target`, docs/air.md) it launches (`0x467ee0`, `0x455520` decrements the stock). OpenSOW: same (`step_ai`, `strike_target`, `launch_strike`).
7. **Turrets** (`0x431120`, `World::ai_build_turret` gathers the inputs, `formats::turretai`, **emulation-verified**: `scripts/verify_against_exe.py` runs the exe's `0x431120` with `rand`, the placement test and the creator stubbed on 1500 random worlds, 0 mismatches, all five tiers incl. the anti-air path; the constructor's eight spots `0x40aa04..0x40ace6` match 200 cases; `opensow turretai|turretspots`): called from `0x42fa3f` / `0x42fa56` right after the air-strike pick, so **once per 600 steps** (the same timer `[0x70f2c8]`; a timer above 0 jumps over it), a second time only while the credits are still >= 200.
   OpenSOW: same; `step_ai` runs the two calls only on the tick the strike timer runs out (`due`; test `the_campaign_computer_builds_turrets_only_when_the_strike_timer_runs_out`). The decision itself (`turretai::plan`) is exact.
   - *Threat* `[b+0x264]` of every own non-turret building: all unit slots (dead ones still in their slot included; the gun tops of finished turrets are units 109-113) whose cell lies in `x-4 .. x+w+3` by `y-4 .. y+h+3`: own owner -1, any other +1, and +1 more for kinds 41-45 and **111-113** (not 109/110).
   - *Free* `[b+0x268]` (only with >= 400 credits): 6 when another side's Advancer (kind 40) stands on the building's action cell (`0x409ab0/0x409ac0`), minus 2 for every one of the building's eight spots that holds an **anti-air turret** (kind 26: its handle type is 6; the other turrets are type 3, `0x40a4e7` / `0x40a4f4`). Otherwise 0.
   - The first building (handle order) whose threat or free value reaches the largest of all of them (running maximum starts at 0) is chosen.
   - *Wanted kind* from credits `c`, threat `t`, free `f`: `c <= 1000`: `t > f`: t<3 none, 3-4 tier 1, 5 tier 3, 6 tier 4, >=7 tier 5; else `f >= 4` tier 2. `1000 < c <= 1500`: `t > f`: t<2 none, 2-3 tier 1, 4 tier 3, 5 tier 4, >=6 tier 5; else `f >= 3` tier 2. `c > 1500`: `t > f`: t<2 none, 2-3 tier 3, >=4 tier 5; else `f >= 2` tier 2.
   - *Step-down* (`0x4316c5..0x43177f`): a tier is built when `c >=` its price (200 x tier) **and the level's flag for that tier is set** (`[player+0x70baf4+tier]`); otherwise 5 -> 4 -> 3 -> **1** (tier 3 skips tier 2); tiers 1 and 2 that fail build nothing.
   - *Spots* (written by the building constructor `0x40aac9..0x40acdd`, `r0` = row of the first blocked cell of the layout): `(x+w-1, y+r0-2)`, `(x+w+1, y+r0)`, `(x+w+1, y+h-1)`, `(x+w-1, y+h+1)`, `(x, y+h+1)`, `(x-2, y+h-1)`, `(x-2, y+r0)`, `(x, y+r0-2)`. Up to eight tries with `rand(8)` (lockstep); the 1x2 turrets (tiers 4, 5) use `y-1`; the first spot that passes the placement test `0x408be0` gets the turret (`0x439e30`, no click sound); the price is taken first.

**Differs from the exe:** skirmishes (not in the exe) add carryall drops (`sim/airlift_ai.rs`; the exe's AI never launches one), spend the air stock where it pays on Hard and Brutal (`sim/air_ai.rs`) and scale thinking, strike period, income and production by difficulty (`Difficulty::handicap`; Normal = the exe's values). Campaign missions keep the exe routines. docs/skirmish.md.

## Economy (`0x42d6b0` tick loop)
- Credits `[0x517af8 + p*4]`, research `[0x517b04 + p*4]` start from level header words **2/3** (credits) and **37/38** (research) of player/enemy (`0x41d9c5`); M01: 400 research, the "400 research points" of the tutorial.
- **Once per second** (the block of `0x42d6b0` only runs on the tick whose in-second index `[0x517b10 + i*4]` is 0) every finished building (`[b+0x68] == 0`: not healing in, not destroyed) adds: gold mine (105) `active + 1` credits (cap 99999), research lab (106) `active + 1` research points (cap 9999) for active = 1..5 (2/3/4... per second; the tutorial text 'two research points in a time unit' matches); when credits cross 200/400/600/800/1000 the sound "new turret" plays, at 500 research "new tech-level".
- Turret prices 200 / 400 / 600 / 800 / 1000 (`0x4172ea`); placement writes a new building (`0x439e30`) that heals in at 150 hp per tick.
- Tech-level (`0x454c19` + `0x40e730`): costs the slot's `+0x1c` price in research (500 ground, 200 others), needs `level < max level` (`slot+0x24`, level words 8-12), turns the slot kind into the next tier (`production::next_tier`: 1-2-3, 4-5-6, ..., 16-17-18 gold, 19-20-21 research, 22-23-24 energy), keeps progress. OpenSOW: same (`tech_up`, `tech_up_all` for the computer).
- Windtrap (107): `[0x5baaa0 + p*4]` = sum of the active slots of the player's windtraps; normal production speed is `30 + 12 * windtrap levels` steps/s (`0x40e640`): 67 s per ground unit without windtrap. In the tutorial the speed is 90 (360 on tutorial steps 15-17).
- Factory exits (constructor `0x40a330`, spawn routine `0x408740`, `production::factory_exit`, exe-tested): the unit always appears inside the footprint (light (+1,+1), others (+2,+1); a unit standing there makes the slot wait). The exits are tested in the static grid `0x5eac38` (terrain and buildings only; units never block an exit and the spawn cell is not checked for units). The unit first walks in a straight line (order type 2, no path finding, through the wall) to the exit cell, then (queued type-1 order) plans a route to the cell two further out. It uses the primary exit, the bottom right cell (light (+3,+3), others (+4,+3)) and on two cells further down; only if that cell is blocked terrain or a building (a turret placed on it) the the alternative top left cell (light (-1,-1), others (+1,0)) and two cells further up. Both occupied: no unit, the slot retries every tick and says "No space for units" (message 5) when its wait counter reaches 240 (`0x40e2ee`).
- OpenSOW: `step_economy` (income, windtrap sum) and `step_production` (`sim/economy.rs`), same rules.

## Footprints and turret placement (`0x409760`, `0x486b20`, `0x408be0`)
`crates/opensow-formats/src/buildings.rs` (exe-tested). Size (w x h) in cells: turrets 25-27 1x1, 28-29 1x2, HQ 4x4, light factory 3x3, medium and heavy 5x4, air base 3x4, gold mine 3x3, research lab 3x3, windtrap 3x4, walker factory 4x3. The layout mask (25 entries, row stride = width) marks the blocked cells; the top row is always open (the HQ blocks rows 1-3, the light factory 5 of its 9 cells, a 1x1 turret its cell). A turret needs every footprint cell free of units, buildings and blocked terrain **and** a building of the same player within two cells of the footprint (`can_place`).

**The placement test `0x408be0(x, y, w, h, player, out[], ok*)`** (exe-tested by `turret_placement_test_reads_the_layers_documented_in_ai_md`): first it scans the rectangle `x-2 .. x+w+2` by `y-2 .. y+h+2` (cells with `0 <= x < W-1`, `0 <= y < H-1` only) in the building-handle grid `0x6f3a4c` (map layer `+0x340cc`) and notes whether a handle's owner (`0x43aa80`) is `player`; the editor flag `[0x517ae9]` forces "near". Then for every footprint cell, row by row, the out entry is 2 (green) when the static grid `0x5eac38` entry is not a ground-unit handle (`0x43aab0` subclass != 1), the walkers' grid `0x6c7a4c` is 0 (terrain, buildings), the **no-build byte** `0x6efa4c` (map layer `+0x300cc`, shore and rock edges) is 0 **and** near holds; else 1 and `*ok = 0`. OpenSOW: `World::is_no_build` carries the layer; `place_turret` and `ai_build_turret` refuse those cells.

**Differs from the exe:** a ground unit blocks through the cell it stands on or heads to; the port tests only the cell it stands on.

## More decoded details
- `[unit+0x4c]` (ctor `0x46bd30`, tables `0x46c13c/0x46c1b4`) = anti-air flag, set for kinds 1, 2, 3, 42 (and 110): these units are the ones the AI sends after enemy Advancers.
- AI air-strike target (`0x435ad0`): an idle enemy pod (kind 46) first; otherwise a 24x20 grid of 128-px blocks: every non-Advancer unit adds to its block (+3), left/right (+2), above/below (+1) for enemies (x2 for an idle walker 41-45) and subtracts 3/2/1 for the AI's own units; the best block is the target if its sum is at least 9 (centre of the block).

## Turrets and their guns (`0x40e004`, jump table `0x40e3b4`; tops decoded from `0x47a000..`)
A turret building gets its gun top only when it has finished healing in (`[bld+0x68]` turns 0): turret kinds 25..29 create the tops **109..113 in order**. Top parameters (`aim::aim_params`, `projectile::weapon_shot`, `recoil::fire_sequence`; exe-tested):
| turret | top | range (cells) | reload (+jitter) | bullet type / strength | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Guard Cannon | 109 | 0-6 | 72 | 6 / 39 | cannon shell |
| 2 A.A. Defence | 110 | 0-9 | 120 (+24) | 1 / 15 | anti-air flag `[+0x4c]`, four rocket tables; shoots aircraft only |
| 3 Plasma Mob | 111 | 0-7 | 39 (+12) | 11 / 40 | |
| 4 LedStorm | 112 | 0-8 | 1 | 7 / 16 | rapid fire (3 steps of 6 ticks), tur_01 flash set |
| 5 | 113 | 2-10 | 70 | 2 / 6, 6 projectiles per shot (`[top+0x30]` = 6) | artillery-like |
Targeted bullets (event types 1, 10, 31: anti-air and flamer shots) do not splash: the event takes its strength off the one target id it carries (an aircraft's `[+0x2c]` hit points, or a unit's `[+0x34]`), so anti-air units reduce aircraft hit points directly.

**Flyer aim** (`0x4797c0`, case `0x4798ac`; `Unit::flyer_points`): for a flying target (`[unit+0x4b]`, the Advancer) `h = table[0x89cf58 + [unit+0x84]*4] + [unit+0x88]` = the hover offset (sprite y minus ground y); the facing aims at `(x, y + h/2)` (truncated) and the shot's end point `[top+0x88]` becomes `(x, y + h)`, i.e. the sprite. The aim origin is the top's anchor (unit position + `[top+0x5c/0x60]`), which for turrets includes the top's vertical offset. Aircraft: the exe aims at `([a+0x18] + [a+4], [a+8] - [a+0x14])` (`[a+0x18]` is the bomber's aim offset, docs/air.md); OpenSOW: same (`World::aircraft_aim_point`).

**Firing effects** (turret tops are units 109-113; `World::turret_muzzle`, tests `a_turret_shell_starts_at_the_muzzle_point_of_the_table`, `the_anti_air_turret_launches_a_missile_...`): a shot fires the `.po1` tables `barrel..barrel+shots-1` of its fire-sequence step (`recoil`), each shell starting at the top's anchor (turret unit position + `[top+0x60]`) minus the table point for the current facing. Flash sprites exist only for turret 1 (`tur_02_fire`, groups 0, 1) and turret 4 (`tur_01_fire`, groups 0..2); turrets 2, 3 and 5 have none. The A.A. turret launches a type-1 `missile` bullet (strength 0, the hit is applied directly as before) from its four rocket tables in the order 0, 2, 1, 3. Turret 5 fires its tables 0, 1, 2 on three steps, 6 rounds each (`[top+0x30]`). **Scatter** (volley spawner `0x47cf40`, `aim::spread` / `aim::rounds`, exe-tested): for each round the end point moves by `+-random(spread)` px on x then y (`random(2) == 1` = plus, then `random(spread)`), the same for all tables of that round; `spread` = `[top+0x20]` = 10 for kinds 1-4 and 6-12, 20 for kind 5, 5 for 13/14, 24 for walker 4 (kind 44), 16 for turret 4, 20 for turret 5, 1 (no movement) for the rest. Skipped in remote games. `World::volley_scatter`.

## Rogon bomb (kind 46) and event type 35 (`0x46df40`, `0x46a4f0`, `0x411220`; `formats::rogon`)
- **Movement**: the pod is an ordinary ground unit (a bare `chN` chassis, no weapon): it chases `[unit+0x7c]` through the general chase `0x46eb40` with weapon range 1 (no weapon), i.e. it walks to the cell next to the target (`wave::approach`) and it **plans around other units like every other ground unit**: traced, the chassis step `0x46f550` -> `0x473190` -> `0x476870` and the chase `0x46eb40` have no branch on kind 46 (the only `cmp .., 0x2e` in the unit code `0x46a000..0x479000` are the target order `0x469fe0`, the death `0x46a4f0` and the touch test `0x46df40`; the UI move orders `0x418a1e` / `0x419088` merely skip pods in missions 3 and 8), so the pod queues behind units, reserves its step cell and walks around corners (the game's `avoid` now runs for it too). In networked games the two-cell planner `0x472f50` applies to it as well (docs/pathfinding.md). Walking speed 30 (7 with period 13 in missions 3 and 8, `anim.rs`). `0x469fe0` (give a target): in missions 3 and 8 a pod ignores attack orders (`0x46a10b`, they are script-driven); for other kinds a cleared flag clears the target, a pod keeps it.
- **Fields**: `0x46df40` runs on the unit object (`[unit+8]` = its chassis, `[unit+0x40]` = kind): every tick it sets the chassis weapon range `[chassis+0x154] = 1` for a pod (the chase distance; other kinds get `(min+max)/2` of their top). `[chassis+8]` is the chassis' **facing step count**: the constructor case of kind 46 (`0x4702fa..0x47031e`, in `0x46ff50`) sets it to **16** (sheet `chN` = index 5, 3 phases `[+0x48]`, animation period `[+0x50]` = 3, speed `[+0x18]` = 30; missions 3 and 8: speed 7, period 13), the frame is `[+0x40] = [+0x44] * [+8] + [+0x30]` (phase * steps + facing, `0x46f4a0`) and the turn step `0x46a190` takes it as the number of facings (the chassis starts at facing `[+0x30]` = 8, which with 16 steps is south). Nothing pod-specific besides the value 16 (the walkers 41-45 also use 16, the tank sheets 32).
- **Touch test** (`0x46df40`, every tick): `[unit+0x34]` (hit points) = 0 when the target handle is found in the static grid `0x5eac38` (index `cx*128+cy`) on **any of the 3 x 3 cells around the bomb's cell** (nine `cmp` at `0x5eaa34..0x5eae3c` = base + 4*(128*dx+dy), `dx,dy` in -1..1; exe-tested). So the bomb detonates one cell away, not on the target. Ground units stamp their handle in that grid on the cell they head to, buildings stamp their footprint; walkers (kinds 41-45) live in the second grid `0x60ac38` that is not tested, so **a bomb never touches a walker**.
- **Death** `0x46a4f0` (for any way the pod's hit points reach 0, shot down too): for kind 46 it only creates the type-35 event `0x411dc0(0x23, 0, x, y, x+20, y+20, owner)` and returns: no death clouds, wreck, flying pieces, puffs or death sound.
- **Event** (`0x411220`): a counter starts at 20 and gains the elapsed ticks each call; while it is above 1 it takes one step: `k++`, counter `-1`, and the event ends when `k >= [0x6b3a34]` (= twice the offset count up to ring 7, entry 7 of the index table `0x6b3a18`), otherwise a blast centre `(x + 16 + 32*w[k], y + 16 + 32*w[k+1])` with `w` the ring offset list of `0x65bbd8` read as overlapping word pairs (a quirk of the exe), area damage `0x42f2c0(.., 370 - k, owner, 35)`, an `e_unit2` cloud (`effects::UNIT_CLOUD`, the unit-death cloud, 29 frames) at the centre + rand(32) - 16 on y then x (`0x4113b0..0x4113f1`; not a fixed blast sprite on the ring cell, which looked blocky) and, when `k % 17 == 1`, a sound (67 if `random(4) == 1`, else 19). The first call fires 20 blasts at once, then one per tick, damage falling from 370 to about 30 over ~5 seconds. HQs are immune to this bullet type.
- Game: right-click on an enemy attacks it (the units chase, `Unit::chase`); the pod explodes when `rogon::touches` (target's cell and heading cell, or building footprint, within the 3 x 3 block); `World::nukes` runs `rogon::Explosion`.

## Headquarters and the Advancer (`0x42e38d`, `0x41e030`)
Each headquarters links an Advancer (`[bld+0xfc]` = its id): at level load every HQ takes the first free Advancer of its side. Every frame a living HQ whose Advancer is gone creates a new one at HQ cell + (1,1) (`0x43a420(.., 0x28, ..)`, sound 9) unless the level header forbids it for that side: header word 34 (player) / 35 (enemy) nonzero = no replacement (M01 enemy, M03/M04/M08 both). `World::step_hq`.
In mission 7 (base game and Warmonger, `0x42e36c` / Warmonger `0x42d6fc`) the player's HQ also waits while the countdown runs: the end flag `[0x5baa99]` (set when the clock shows 0:00, `0x42ec11`) lets it build its Advancer, which is the Advancer the Sudan briefing promises with the reinforcements (neither M07 places one). OpenSOW: same (`step_hq` waits for `timer_expired`); test `in_mission_7_the_player_gets_an_advancer_only_when_the_countdown_ends`. The exe also skips the rule outside the campaign and skirmish game modes (`[0x70f2cc]` not -1/0x18/0x19, network games): not ported, OpenSOW has no such mode.
Shipped levels and their Advancers (marker counts, word 34/35): no level places a marker for a side whose word is set, so the loader skip (docs/levels.md) only matters for custom maps. Player without any Advancer: base M03, M04, M08, M11, M12, M17, M18; Warmonger m02, m03, m12, m22; base M07 and Warmonger m07 until the countdown ends. Warmonger files are named by land, not by the briefing's TERRITORY number: m07 is Territory 06, Territory 07 is m11 (docs/warmonger.md).

## Convoy missions 3 and 8 (`0x42e580`, `0x4212f0`)
The player's Rogon pods are driven by script: mission 3 sends every pod to (176, 2352) px each frame, mission 8 sends pods west of x 2880 and south of y 352 to (2960, 272). Both lose as soon as the player has no pod; mission 3 has no other rule and is won when a pod stands on cells x 4..6, y 72..74 (the game does exactly this, `World::step_convoy`, `check_outcome`).

## Order target beats the target priority (`0x47b140`)
A unit's weapon takes its target from the ground scan `0x47b140(x, y, &[unit+0x7c], &[top+0x7c], min, max, ...)`. `[unit+0x7c]` is the unit's order target (attack order `0x469fe0`, AI order). During the scan over the range rings every object handle whose side is hostile **or equals the order target** (`0x47b232`: the side is forced to "enemy" for it) is collected into a unit list and a building list; when the order target is among them the flag `bl` is set (`0x47b27c`, `0x47b2a2`) and the function ends by storing the order target as the weapon target (`0x47b2b3..0x47b2c1`), whatever else is in range. Only without an order target in range does the priority run: the first unit of the list, else the first building of class 3 or 6 (turrets), else the first building (`0x47b364..0x47b3b2`). `World::find_target(.., order)`; tests `units_shoot_what_they_were_ordered_to_attack_when_it_is_in_range`, `a_unit_in_range_of_both_prefers_its_order_target_over_the_priority_rule`.

**Neutral buildings as AI targets (checked again in the exe):** the target flood fill `0x472a90` skips an object only when its owner field (handle bits 16-18, helper `0x43aa80`) equals the AI's own player (`cmp eax,[ebp+0x10]; je` at `0x472be6`), so neutral buildings (owner 2) are valid targets. Only strategy 1 goes straight for the HQ; strategies 2-4 pick the nearest turret / unit / *other building* (any non-own, neutral included), so enemy units walking to a neutral building and shooting it is the original's behaviour. What the port got wrong (fixed) was keeping that building as a lasting chase target until it fell: in the exe the order is one route and the target is cleared at once (item 4), so the units move on at the next strategy re-roll (900..1500 ticks).


## Update order (`0x42da61..0x42ddfe`)
Per tick the economy block runs first (`0x42d742..0x42d9ff`), then player 0, 1 and 2 in turn: the local input or the computer (`0x42dc22`); the player's buildings in slot order, twice (`0x40d9a0`, then `0x40de60`: heal-in payout, destruction animation, production, and a healed-in turret creates its gun unit; a building that returns false is rebuilt by `0x43ab40` or, for turrets and headquarters, removed by `0x43a640`); its aircraft (table `0x5ba910 + p*30`, `0x467b50`); its units (table `0x5b62c0 + p*1000`, `0x46df40`; turret guns are units of this table; a dead unit keeps its slot as a corpse and is removed by `0x43a7e0` when the corpse ends); its bullets (table `0x5b91a0 + p*500`, `0x411220`). Every table is walked by slot; a new object takes the first free slot (`0x43a50c`, `0x43a147`, `0x43a316`), and removal clears the slot and trims trailing empty ones. So a unit created early in a turn can be updated in the same turn, and the lockstep draws come in this order.
Before the turns the tick loop runs the mission timers and rewards (`0x42ebd0`), the mission scripts (`0x42e580`: the pods of missions 3 and 8) and the per-second economy, then the tutorial script (`0x4325b0`, per frame). Inside a player's turn, the second building pass also runs the satellite protection (`0x409f50`, called at `0x40e38e`); the Advancer's beam action is part of its unit update (chassis tick `0x46f210..0x46f321`); the Advancer's shots (bullet types 19..21) and the Rogon bomb's event (type 35) are objects of the owner's bullet table and take their step at their slot among the bullets. After the turns (`0x42de0b..0x42e412`) come the wrecks (`0x45b0a0`), the sprite lists (`0x4050d0`), the clouds, the win/lose check (`0x421230`) and the headquarters' replacement Advancers (`0x42e380..0x42e412`).
OpenSOW: `World::step` and `World::player_turn` (`sim/turns.rs`) in this order: `step_mission`, `step_convoy`, `step_economy`, `step_tutorial`; per player the computer, buildings (heal-in, destruction animation, production, gun creation, `satellite_check`), aircraft, units with their `advancer_action`, guns and corpses in slot order, then `bullet_turn` (flights first, as they draw only from the visual generator, then landings, Advancer shots and bomb steps in slot order); afterwards `step_wrecks`, blinks, decals and sprites, `check_outcome` (every tick; it used to run every 20), `step_hq`. Slots: `Unit::slot`, `World::gun_slot`, `Corpse::slot` (one table per player), `Bullet::slot`, `BeamShot::slot`, `Nuke::slot` (one bullet table per owner, `push_bullet` / `push_beam` / `push_nuke` take the first free slot at creation), `Aircraft::slot` (first free at the side's turn, which is when the exe's table would have it too); savegames keep every slot (the handles' slot bits, the record keys). Test `a_new_object_takes_the_first_free_slot`.
**Differs from the exe:** the exe runs the computer, the tutorial script and the after-turn block once per rendered frame (also on frames with no tick, `0x42d020` runs flat out), the port once per tick; the per-tick parts (strategy timer, strike timer, the objects' updates) are the same, but an Advancer that stays idle draws its `rand(3)` per building on every frame in the exe.
**Differs from the exe:** the building blinks (`0x4086a0`) and the decals' clean-up run after the turns; neither draws from the lockstep table.
