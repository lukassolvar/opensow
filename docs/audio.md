# Audio (decoded tables)

OpenSOW plays every sound through `rodio`: effects and speech in `opensow-game/src/sfx.rs`, music in `music.rs`, optionally improved by `hifi.rs`. The decoded tables are in `formats::sfx` / `formats::messages`.
**Differs from the exe:** the exe plays through DirectSound buffers (volume and pan in hundredths of a dB). OpenSOW converts the same dB values to linear gains (`sfx::gains`) and mixes them in rodio.

- Files: `Data/Sounds/{Music,SFX,Speech,Tutorial}` (459 PCM wavs). Music: `Menu`, `Brief`, `Brief2`, `Statistic`, `uvod` (intro) and `Level01..13`. When a mission starts (flag `[0x49a339] == 1` in `0x42d6b0`) the game plays one of `Level01..Level04` chosen with the visual random generator `0x437cd0(4)` (jump table `0x42e548`); the other level tracks are used elsewhere. Tutorial voice: `Tutorial/NN.wav` per tutorial step (docs/tutorial.md).
- **Speech**: the 24 message events (docs/text.md, `formats::messages`) plus `01-game-saved`, `01-mis-complet`, `01-mis-failed`, `05-airforce-destroyed`.
- **Sound effects**: `0x407470(path, id)` loads 72 effects at start-up (loader `0x406dc0..0x407453`), `0x4075b0(id, x, y, ...)` / `0x4077e0` play them (up to eight voices at a time, panned by map position). The id table is `formats::sfx::SFX` (exe-tested; ids 31 `mainmenu-fly` and 49 `airicon-come` are loaded by the exe but missing from the shipped data, 71 is unused): 0 anti-air, 1-3 artillery, 4-6 cannon, 7-9 special, 10 flamer, 11-15 turret guns 1-5, 16-19 walker 1/3/5/nuke, 20-22 `strelba` (fire) of anti-air/artillery/cannon, 23-28 explosions (air unit, building, building2, sentry, unit, unit-turret), 29-48 menu / in-game / briefing / statistic interface sounds, 49-61 icon, info-box, turret-tool (`tt-capture` 53, `tt-click` 54, `tt-dest` 55, `tt-fire` 56, `tt-repair` 57, `tt-Upgrade` 58) and unit interface sounds, 62-70 air forces (carryall landing, bomber `hercules*`, fighter `stihac*`, `airforce-dest`, `tripler-fire`, `meteor`), 72 `turret-place`.
- The Advancer's order plays 55 (`0x46e5c0/620/680`), its beam start 53 / 57 / 58 for destroy / repair / upgrade (`0x4111b0`).
- Firing sounds per weapon: see "Muzzle sounds" below (`sfx::muzzle`). Bullet-type sounds: the **constructor** `0x411dc0` plays one per bullet created, at its start point with pitch jitter (`effects::constructor_sound`, exe-tested): type 28 (special tier 2) -> 8, type 29 (special tier 3) -> 9, type 31 (Advancer gun) -> 56; the **impact** function `0x40f590` plays one where the bullet lands (`effects::impact_sound`), including the `strelba` effects 20-22 for bullet types 1, 3/4, 6/7 (cases `0x40f5ff..0x40f61b` push the id and jump to one shared call `0x40f665` -> `0x4077e0`, no jitter). An earlier note here called 20-22 unused: its scan read only the last push before each call, which at the shared call is the meteor's 70. Voice limit and pan: next sections.

## Music (`wav.rs`, `opensow-game/src/music.rs`)
Nothing to decode: every track is a plain RIFF PCM wav (`wav::parse`; 22050 Hz, mono or stereo, about 80 minutes in all, `every_music_track_is_plain_pcm`).
The exe's music player is `0x407bb0(name)` (stop: `0x408550`). Calls found: `menu.wav` on the main menu (`0x433cb4`), `brief.wav` / `brief2.wav` on the
campaign map (`0x441c72/0x441d20`, chosen by `0x437cd0(2)`), `Level01..04` at mission start (random, `0x42d6e0`; Level05..13 are named in the player's
track list `0x406a00..0x407d50` but no other caller was traced), `statistic.wav` on the results screen (`0x44d8ac`), `uvod.wav` on the boot pictures (`CMenuCopyright` update `0x442400` and constructor `0x4426e0`; also the three promo pictures `CMenuCoolStuff`, `0x4421dd..`), restarted at every page (docs/game.md).
The game loops the current track (looping is assumed) and plays these for the menu, the map and missions; movies stop it; `--mute` / `--no-intro` silence it.

## Effects in the game (`opensow-game/src/sfx.rs`)
Effect ids from `formats::sfx::SFX`.
**Differs from the exe:** there are two voice pools instead of the exe's eight shared voices. World sounds (`Sfx::play_at`: shots, explosions, aircraft, impacts) get 32 voices (`WORLD_VOICES`) and a new one is still dropped when all play; the player's own action and interface sounds (`Sfx::play`: clicks, orders, selection, air strike launch, rally point, turret placed, info box, menus) get 16 more (`PRIORITY_VOICES`), are unpositioned (centred, distance ignored) and never dropped - when that pool is full its oldest voice stops.
**Differs from the exe:** the sim raises the local player's strike launch (68) and turret placement (72) in `World::player_sounds` instead of the positional queue (the exe plays them at the target / cell, so a strike called far off screen was silent there).
**Differs from the exe:** a click on any building (own or not) plays 54 `tt-click` (`play::BUILDING_CLICK_SOUND`), which the exe does not: its building select (`0x417be0` -> `0x43ad60`) only sounds when units of that group join the selection.
Wired (OpenSOW plays these where the exe does): main menu hover 30 `mainmenu-move` and click 29 `mainmenu-ok` (call sites `0x445d1d`, `0x446061`;
options 33/34), campaign map 41 on hovering a land, 46+41+44 on selecting one, 43 per four typed characters, 29 on ACCEPT (the pushes in `0x43ebe0`),
in-game muzzle sounds (`sfx::muzzle`, with the +-10 % pitch jitter), explosions (units: one of 23/24/26/27, buildings 24, turrets 28, aircraft 23), turret placed 72,
unit select 60, attack order 59 (`0x417b82`/`0x417783`), move order 61 `unit-dest` (the group go-to method `0x418220`, `0x41849b`: for the local player it also drops a marker animation, sprite array `0x5e6140`, and plays the effect non-positionally), the Advancer's capture / repair / upgrade order 55 `tt-dest` (`0x46e5c0` mode 0x15 and its two siblings, `0x46b8b0..0x46bbf7`; earlier notes had mistaken it for the move order), air strike launch 68 `airforce-dest` at the target (`0x417221`, marker sprite `0x5e5fb0`; `0x417636` is a second path, marker `0x5e6028`, non-positional), selecting the whole army (the group select `0x43ad60`): 54 `tt-click` once per Advancer (`0x43ae51`), 60 once if any other unit is in it. Only world sounds near the screen are played (player sounds always are).
Speech: every message event (`formats::messages`, `0x402ec0`) speaks its wav through one stream (`0x407f30` on the audio object `0x6b3d38`); the game lets a new line cut off the old one. `01-mis-complet` / `01-mis-failed` play when the mission ends (`0x4358a0` / `0x4358d5`, the mission-end function picks by the outcome code 9 = lost). `01-game-saved` (`0x429018`) plays after a save (`game_screens.rs`); `05-airforce-destroyed` (no reference in the exe) is not played; event 6 has no file. Events 11/12 also blip `infobox-income` (51) and 18-22 `airicon-come` (49) - the names fit, the exe's call sites (`0x40e701`, `0x4277c8..0x428056` push 49; 51 and 52 are in the HUD code) were not traced to their events.
More in missions: Advancer beam sounds (`0x4111b0`: mode 0x13 repair 57, 0x14 upgrade 58, 0x15 destroy 53) on every third shot; the impact sounds of `effects::impact_sound` (`0x40f590`; the bomb 18 only half the time, meteors with pitch jitter); the nuke ring rumbles on every 17th blast (19, now and then 67, `0x411386`); aircraft (`Aircraft::sounds`): bomber drone 63 every 9 steps (`0x467bde`), carryall landing 62 once within 190 px and above 45 (`0x467592`), fighter roar 66 (`0x46765d`), tripler pod 69 per shot (`0x46843a`).
**Differs from the exe:** the exe gates the fighter roar on the view; OpenSOW plays it once while the fighter closes in (`Aircraft::sounds`, an approximation).
Wired since the HUD: icon hover 50, info box open 51 / close 52 (`infobox.rs`). Traced earlier: icon hover 50 (`0x415c2f`, `0x4416c3`, icon-bar code); the info box `0x43dfd0(mode)` (modes 0-6, old mode in `[+0x24]`): entering mode 1 from another mode plays 51 `infobox-income`, entering mode 6 from a mode other than 0 plays 52 `infobox-outcome` (`0x43e066..0x43e0b7`; set to 1 at `0x455b4c`, to 2/5 elsewhere, silent). `tt-fire` 56 is played by the bullet constructor `0x411dc0` for bullet type 31 (`0x412240`, at the start point, positional); type 31 is the Advancer's gun (docs/art.md), so every gun shot plays it, jittered, at the shot's start point (`GUN_SOUND` in `sim.rs`); the volley spawner's muzzle switch has no case for kind 40 (byte table entry 17 = silent). Not played because the exe does not: `05-airforce-destroyed`; `01-game-saved` is played after a save. The tutorial voices `Tutorial\NN.wav` play on the first frame of each step (`Play::tut_voices`); their exact timing against the panel is unchecked.

## Voice limit (`0x4075b0`)
The player needs the three audio switches (`[+0x4c]`, `[+0x4d]`, `[+0x5e]`) and a loaded buffer for the id, and bails out when the volume is 0xffff (too far) or -10000. It then looks for a free
slot among eight (`[+0x64 + 0x20 * i]`, null = free); with none free it asks every slot's buffer for its status (vtable `+0x24`): the first slot whose buffer no longer plays is released and
reused; if all eight are still playing the **new sound is dropped** (the game keeps this for world sounds with 32 voices, `WORLD_VOICES`; player sounds have their own pool). The buffer is a duplicate of the loaded one (`0x44da70`), volume and pan set, played once
(or looped with arg 5). Pitch jitter: `freq/100*10` is the span, `rand(2)` the sign (visual generator `0x437cd0`).

## Pan and distance (`0x4075b0`, helpers `0x406510` pan, `0x406550` volume)
**Pan quirk**: `0x406510` subtracts the fixed 0x140 (320), not half the screen width, so with a window wider than 640 a sound in the middle of the screen leans right (1024 px: 6 * 192 = +1152, about 11.5 dB off the left channel). The game now passes `x - viewX - 320` (`Sfx::play_at`'s `pan_dx`); the distance volume still uses the real screen centre. In the end the pan is not used at all (next paragraphs).
Units are DirectSound hundredths of a dB. Pan = `6 * (x - viewX - 320)` clamped to +-4000 (0 for an unpositioned call, x = -1). Volume: `base = 30 * (master - 100)` (master `[audio+0x58]`, 0..100; master 0 = -10000 = not played); with a position, `d = trunc(sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2))` from the screen centre (`viewX + W/2`, `viewY + H/2`, W/H = `[0x49a340]`/`[0x49a344]`), `loss = max(0, 3d - 500)`; `loss >= 1500` (d >= 667) returns 0xffff and the sound is not played; else `volume = clamp(base - loss, -10000, 0)`. `sfx::pan` / `sfx::volume` / `sfx::gains` port this (read from the disassembly, unit-tested on hand-computed values, not emulated); the game mixes the effects stereo with those gains (`Sfx::play_at`), master volume = the options menu's FX volume (`Sfx::fx_volume`; speech uses the VOICE volume).

## Muzzle sounds
`sfx::muzzle(kind)` (exe-tested for kinds 1..113). The volley spawner `0x47cf40` (the function that creates a volley's bullets, docs/projectiles.md) plays one effect at the fire point after the bullets: it switches on the weapon kind (`[esi+8] - 1`, byte table `0x47d28c`, jump table `0x47d244`) and calls `0x4075b0(id, x, y, pitch_jitter, loop)`. Kinds 1-3 and 42 (anti-air; Walker 2 reuses it) -> 0; 4/5/6 artillery -> 1/2/3; 7/8/9 cannon -> 4/5/6; 10-12 flamer -> 10; 13 special tier 1 -> 7; 41/43/45 walkers 1/3/5 -> 16/17/18; turrets 109-113 -> 11-15. Silent here: special tiers 2-3 (kinds 14, 15: their sounds 8 / 9 come from the bullet constructor instead, once per bullet, see above), Walker 4 (44) and all other kinds. `pitch_jitter` (arg 4) shifts the playback frequency by a random +-10 % (`rand(2)` sign, `rand(freq/10)`; off for walkers 1 and 3); arg 5 is the DirectSound looping flag (always 0 here). `0x4077e0(id, ...)` is the same player with id -1 meaning a random unit explosion (23, 24, 26, 27 via `rand(4)` -> `0x17,0x18,0x1a,0x1b`).

**Differs from the exe:** centred sound. `Sfx::play_at` ignores the pan (`pan_dx`) and plays every world sound in the middle, keeping only the distance volume; the exe's pan above stays documented and ported (`sfx::pan`). With the pan, players on headphones heard a crackle just before regular tanks fired and when Walker 1 shots landed (2026-11-09; the raw wavs and an offline render were clean); centring made it go away. The exact mechanism was not found.

**Differs from the exe:** hearing radius. `Sfx::play_at` scales the distance from the view centre by 4/9 before `sfx::volume`, so world sounds are heard out to about 1500 px instead of the exe's 667 px (with a gentler fade); pan is unchanged.

## Music after an audio device error (not in the exe)
Each track opens its own output stream (`intro::Sound::start_pcm`). rodio keeps "playing" into a stream that died (the output device
was switched or unplugged, the machine slept), so the music goes silent for good. The stream's error callback now sets
`Sound::failed` (and prints `audio stream error: ...`); `Music::tick`, called at the start of every `Game::update`, then opens a new
stream and starts the wanted track again (`music: the audio device failed, reopening it`), at most every 3 seconds. Not yet seen
in the wild: it is the one plausible cause of "music stopped mid-game" found by reading the code (the loop itself was checked: a
looping buffer keeps playing past its length). Sound effects and speech (`sfx.rs`) have their own stream and no such recovery.

**Device buffer** (not in the exe): rodio asks the output for a 2048-frame buffer; Bluetooth headphones (AirPods accept 15..=960) reject it and the game was silent. `intro::open_output` tries 2048, 960, 512, 256, then the device default (`OPENSOW_AUDIO_BUFFER=N` tries N first); effects, speech and music all open the device through it.

**Debug tap** (not in the exe): `OPENSOW_AUDIO_DUMP=out/mix.raw` records the effects and speech mix as it goes to the device (after the limiter; raw 16-bit little-endian, device rate and channels) and prints a `sfx <time>s id ...` line for every effect that starts, to compare what is heard with what was played.

## Sound enhancement (`opensow-game/src/hifi.rs`)
The audio sibling of the texture enhancement (docs/enhance.md): computed while playing, nothing is stored, the original wavs stay untouched.
**Differs from the exe:** with the switch on (the default), effects, speech, music and movie sound are resampled, faded and limited as described below, so they do not sound exactly like the exe. With it off they play as plain rodio output.
Options page 2 has the switch ("Enhanced sound", `enhance_sound` in `opensow.cfg`, on by default; docs/options.md); `--no-sound-enhance` / `OPENSOW_SOUND_ENHANCE=0` force it off for a run. Off plays exactly as before (rodio's own conversion, no fades, no limiter). Switching it while the game runs rebuilds the effects route (`Sfx::open`), cutting the effects still playing; music changes path at its next track.
- **Sinc resampling**: rodio converts the 22050 Hz sounds to the device rate (44.1 / 48 kHz) by linear interpolation, which leaves mirror
  images of the sound above 11 kHz (gritty on headphones). `hifi::Resampled` is a streaming Kaiser-windowed sinc (16 zero crossings a side,
  beta 8.6, cutoff 0.95 of the lower Nyquist) that plays at the device rate, so rodio converts nothing; the pitch jitter (`speed`) is folded
  into it. Used for effects, speech, music (seamless loop) and movie soundtracks when their rate differs from the device's. Measured on the
  tank, cannon and Walker 1 shots at 48 kHz: energy above 11.5 kHz 0.002-0.04 % with linear (about -34..-47 dB), 0.00000 % with the sinc
  (`hifi` tests: a 5 kHz sine converts within 0.002 of exact, 20x better than linear). Cost: 48 voices about 3 ms per 20 ms buffer
  (worst 3.7 ms), a typical fight well under 1 ms.
- **Fades**: 2 ms in and 3 ms out on every effect and speech line (`hifi::prepare`); many files start or end mid-waveform
  (`Canon/01.wav` begins at 1484), which clicks.
- **Limiter**: effects and speech play into their own mixer whose sum goes through rodio's limiter (-1 dB threshold, 4 dB knee, 5 ms attack,
  100 ms release) before the device: a recorded battle clipped 505 samples, 347 in one moment, without it. Music has its own stream and no limiter.
