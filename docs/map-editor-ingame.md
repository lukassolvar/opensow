# In-game map editor and custom skirmish maps (not in the original)

Players make their own skirmish maps in the game, keep them in a folder they can share, and play them from the **Custom** tab of the skirmish setup screen. The internal balance editor (docs/map-editor.md) stays a separate dev tool; both share the editing state (`opensow_viewer::editor::Editor`).

## Entry points
- **Main menu**: row MAP EDITOR after SKIRMISH (`menu::Action::MapEditor`, label `tr.editor_row()`; eight rows now, 52 px apart at 1080p). It opens the editor's start page.
- **Skirmish setup screen** (`skirmish_ui.rs`): the map list has two tabs, **Original** (the shipped `.net` maps) and **Custom** (the maps folder, read again each time the tab opens: `SkirmishMenu::set_tab`). Wide layout: the tabs sit in the list's heading, **Open folder** and **Edit** under the list; compact: a tab row above the map selector with a folder button and Edit. A file that does not load, or a map with a side without a headquarters, shows its row in orange with `!` and the reason in the picture's place; START does nothing then. Custom maps earn no stars: the Custom tab shows no star badge, no row stars and no star note, and a win records none. A custom map's lobby defaults are its own resources (`Setup::of_map`: no carryall rule, the maker chose them).
- **Edit** opens the map in the editor; leaving the editor then returns to the Custom tab.

## The start page
Backdrop and bars like the skirmish screen (design 1920x1080 or 1024x768, `mapeditor_ui::Geo`). Left **YOUR MAPS** (the folder's maps, scrollable with the wheel; Open, Delete (asks once more: the button then reads "Sure? Delete"; selecting another map disarms it), Open folder; headless `--save-map` saves the open map); right **NEW MAP**: the terrains as thumbnails of their ground (`scene::thumbnail`, loaded a few per frame), tabs State of War / Warmonger when Warmonger is installed, and a last dashed tile **Generated terrain - coming later** reserved for the map generator. The bottom bar: BACK, the chosen terrain's mission and size, CREATE.

## The editor page
The map fills the window left of a sidebar (480 design px wide, 360 compact; `map_width`). Map: the viewer's renderer (`render::render`) with the editor's marks (`Editor::draw_marks`: HQ rings, hover / selection frames, green / red placement ghost). Drag on empty ground pans, the wheel zooms around the pointer, 8 % a notch eased in over a few frames (`ZOOM_STEP`, `ZOOM_EASE`), never further out than the whole map (the start view: `fit_zoom`), the arrows and W A S D scroll smoothly while held, at the Options' KEYS SCROLL speed like the game's map; drag an object to move it. A switch on the map's top-left corner (key B) tints where nothing can be built: impassable ground red, the no-build edges orange (the `.map` grid, the rules of `Editor::can_place`); it shows by itself while a building or turret is armed. Some terrains leave little room: Warmonger's M22 has 62 % of its cells impassable, including open-looking ground (the base game's terrains: 33-63 %).

Sidebar, top: title and save state (Not saved yet / Unsaved changes / Saved), the **name field**, "Saves as <file>", **Save**, **Undo**, **Redo**, **Back** (asks once when there are unsaved changes), a message line (refusals, saved, overwrite question).

Nothing selected:
- **PLACE**: owner chips (Neutral / Blue / Green), tabs Buildings / Turrets / Units, the palette as the game's sprites in the owner's colours (`page::thing_picture`). Click a picture to arm it, click the map to place (stays armed), click the picture again / right click / Esc to stop. Units and headquarters are never neutral (armed with Neutral chosen, the owner switches to Blue). The Advancer is not in the palette: it belongs to a headquarters (below).
- **SIDES** (blue | green): credits (step 500, max 19000), research (step 100, max 2000), **Advancer** `n/HQs` (only shown: each headquarters' own switch turns its Advancer on or off), the five air forces (0-20) and the five turret tiers, with the skirmish screen's pictures.
- **MISSION**: a countdown and the win rule ("Missions" below).
- When the window is too short for all the cards, chips switch between them.
- **Checks**: "Ready to play", or per side "no headquarters" (blocks a match) / "no Advancer, cannot capture" (warning only).

An object selected (click it; Done, Esc, right click or a click on empty ground leaves): its picture, name from the language file, cell, **Delete** (never a side's last headquarters), **Done**, **FACTION** chips (units and headquarters: Blue / Green), and for buildings and turrets **START HEALTH** (5-100 %, step 5, with the hit points and a Default button: 100 % owned, 50 % neutral). Buildings (not the headquarters, which the exe always protects: constructor `0x40a47a`) and turrets: **SATELLITE PROTECTION** On / Off (language string 145): object word 15, which the exe's loader copies to `[b+0x114]` for every object without a kind check (`0x41dffc`); turrets run the same building update whose tail calls the satellite check (`0x40e383` -> `0x409f50`), so a protected turret kills attacking Advancers in the original too (docs/advancer-actions.md). It is original data, no record in the port's block (`Editor::set_protected`). OpenSOW: same (`sim/setup.rs` sets `Structure::protected` from word 15 for buildings and turrets, `sim/advancer_actions.rs` runs the satellite check). Turrets: **TYPE** 1-5 as pictures (a type whose footprint does not fit is refused). Headquarters: **ADVANCER** On / Off. Factories, air base, walker factory: **PRODUCTION**: per line its HUD icon (`extras\\ikony` frame = line kind), name, tier chip (T1, T1-2, T1-3, T2, T2-3, T3), `<` `>` kind, the **lock** (unlocked at the start / unlocked by the Advancer; lines unlock in order, so a click locks a line with those after it or unlocks it with those before it: `LineChange::Lock`) and, while locked, the **upgrade points** it needs (step 500, 500-20000; the default is the game's 2000 ground / 4000 air), remove, **Add line** (five at most). Gold mine, lab, windtrap: **LEVELS** with the same rows (Add level up to three, remove the last). A short hint explains the lock. A card taller than the window scrolls with the wheel.

Keys: B (where nothing can be built), Esc (stop placing, deselect, Back), Delete / Backspace (delete the selection), Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Y, Ctrl/Cmd+S, arrows or W A S D (pan). In the name field: typed text (`textinput.rs`; the window's composed text, so dead keys and the Czech / Slovak layouts work, and input-method commits: the IME is enabled while the field has focus), Backspace, Delete, Left / Right, Home / End; Enter, Tab or Esc leave the field. Paste is not supported (no clipboard access yet).

Everything is translated in `tr.rs` (EN, SK, CZ, CS, CT); names of buildings, turrets, units, production levels and air forces, CREDITS / RESEARCH and the Advancer come from the language files (`LangFile` indices: units 173-187, walkers 188-192, Advancer 193, Rogon bomb 194, turrets 195-199, air forces 200-204, buildings 205-213, levels 219-227).

## Terrain
A map's ground is one of the 22 campaign missions' terrains of the base game, or of Warmonger when it is installed (`custom_maps::terrains`, `terrain_source`: the `GameDir` edition picks the install). It fixes the size, water, cliffs and no-build cells. Level header word 1 holds the mission, the port's block the edition. The generator's tile is a placeholder: a generated terrain will need its own record in the block.

## Map name and file name
- The name keeps what was typed: control characters dropped, spaces collapsed, at most 32 characters (`custommap::clean_name`).
- The file: `custommap::file_stem` folds diacritics (`Žltá púšť` -> `zlta-pust`), turns every other character that is not an ASCII letter or digit into `-`, collapses and trims dashes, lower case, at most 32; a name with nothing left (Chinese, symbols) becomes `map-` + six hex digits of a hash of the name. Extension `.sowmap` (the game's `.map` is its terrain grid).
- Saving over the file of another map warns once ("... exists: Save again to replace it"); a renamed map saves to its new file and leaves the old one.

## Maps folder
`maps` in the per-user data folder (`launcher::data_dir()`, next to `skirmish-stars.txt`), created on the first save; `OPENSOW_MAPS_DIR` overrides it (tests, headless demos). Open folder shows it in the system's file manager (`open` / `explorer` / `xdg-open`). Players share maps by copying files.

## File format (`formats::custommap`)
A complete level script (`Level::to_bytes`, byte-identical for every shipped `.edt` / `.net`: `tests/real_data.rs`) followed by the port's block:

```text
"OSOW", u16 version 1, u16 record count
records: u16 tag, u32 length, payload
  1 NAME     the name as typed (UTF-8)
  2 TERRAIN  u8 edition (0 State of War, 1 Warmonger)
  3 OBJECT   u32 object index, i32 x5 upgrade points per line (0 = the game's own), u32 flags (bit 0: no Advancer)
  4 MISSION  u8 win rule (0 no enemy headquarters, 1 nothing of the enemy left, 2 escort)
  5 REINFORCEMENT  i32 credits, i32 research, u8 x5 air forces (header order), u16 count, count x (u16 cell x, u16 cell y, u8 squad 0-17)
  6 ESCORT   u8 goal set, u16 goal x, u16 goal y, u16 count, count x (u16 cell x, u16 cell y) waypoints in order (at most 8)
```

The MISSION record is written only when it differs from the default (the headquarters rule), REINFORCEMENT and ESCORT only when they hold something; a reader takes the bytes it knows and ignores further ones.

Unknown tags are skipped. The original's loader (`0x41da00`, an MFC `CArchive` read) loops over the record tags until the footer `0x1de8` and returns (`0x41da39` -> `0x41e01b` links each HQ to an Advancer, `0x41e0d2` returns): nothing reads past the footer, so the level part alone is a valid `.net` and the block is ignored by the original (read from the disassembly; not emulated). `Level::parse` still demands that a level file ends at its footer; `Level::parse_prefix` reads the level at the start of a custom map.

Advancers are written the original's way (`custommap::sync_advancers`): one Advancer marker per headquarters that has one, at HQ cell + (1,1) where the exe places a replacement, and header word 34 / 35 set for a side none of whose headquarters has one. The editor works without Advancer markers (they are derived; `MapEditor::open` drops a file's).

## Beyond the original (skirmish only)
- **Differs from the exe:** neutral start health: the exe starts every neutral object at exactly half (`0x43a003`); a skirmish starts them at their word 18 percent (`World::neutral_start_hp_from_level`; the shipped maps hold 50 there after `skirmish::even_start_health`, so they are unchanged).
- **Differs from the exe:** upgrade points per line: the exe unlocks a slot when the upgrade points reach its production target (`0x40e3d0`); `SlotState::unlock` overrides that (`unlock_points`, also the HUD's upgrade bar and the Advancer AI's estimate).
- **Differs from the exe:** Advancer power is always 10 for both sides (`custommap::ADVANCER_POWER`, header words 31/32, set when a file is read and written; the shipped maps use 6..15). The editor does not offer it; the exe uses whatever the header holds.
- **Differs from the exe:** Advancers per headquarters: the exe gives every headquarters an Advancer and replaces a lost one (`0x42e38d`) unless word 34/35 is set; `World::hq_without_advancer` (from the block, `World::apply_custom`) keeps a headquarters without one, at the start (its Advancer goes to a headquarters of the side that has none) and for good. Any number of headquarters per side is the original's own rule (a side loses when it has none left).

## Missions (custom maps only)
A custom map can be a mission instead of a plain skirmish (`custommap::Mission`, `Timer`). In: the file format, the rules in the sim, the editor's MISSION card, reinforcements, escort missions and the objective line on the skirmish screen.
- **The editor's MISSION card** (`MapEditor::mission`, nothing selected): **Countdown** None / Hold out / Time limit / Reinforcements, **Minutes** (5-95 in steps of 5, a new countdown starts at 20; no stepper without a countdown), with Reinforcements **Arrives at 0:00** (credits and research in steps of 500 up to 20000, the five air forces 0-20, **Landing points** n / 6: a row per point with its number and cell, the **squad** it brings (`<` `>` through the game's eighteen squads, `aircraft::squad`, shown as "Squad 1".."Squad 18" with the units' pictures and counts below; Squad 18 is two walkers), a cross that deletes it, and *Add on map*), **To win** Enemy HQ / Everything / Escort; with Escort: **Convoy** (the blue pods on the map, "3 × Rogon Bomb", or a hint to place them from the Units tab), **Goal** (its cell, *Set on map*), **Route** n / 8 (*Add on map* adds waypoints in order until eight, *Remove last*). *Add on map* arms the map: each click adds a landing point until six are set (right click, Esc or the button again lets go), bringing the squad after the previous point's (the first: Squad 1); the block under the pointer shows green where it may go and red where not. A point is **refused** unless a whole squad can land: all of its 4x2 cells (`custommap::landing_block`: x-1..x+2, y..y+1) inside the map, on passable ground and off every building's and turret's footprint (`Editor::can_land`), and none shared with another point (`custommap::landings_overlap`). A point that an edit covers later (a building placed on it) or that a hand-made file holds is drawn orange, its row's number turns orange and the checks card says "Landing point N: blocked ground or a building" / "overlaps another" (a warning: the carryall still flies and lands the units that fit). The map shows each point as the 4x2 cells the carryall fills with a ring on the cell it aims at. Shown alone behind its chip, a card taller than the window scrolls with the wheel. Where the sidebar holds all three cards they are stacked (2560x1440); where only the palette and the sides fit, two chips switch `PLACE · SIDES` / `MISSION` (1920x1080); below that three chips PLACE / SIDES / MISSION (`Hit::Content`). The countdown is part of the level header, so Undo takes it back; the win rule, the reinforcements and the escort's goal and route are not in the undo history. Headless: `--content mission`, and `--timer`, `--win`, `--reinforce CREDITS,RESEARCH[,bombers,asteroids,carryalls,triplers,fighters]`, `--drops "CX,CY[,SQUAD];.."`, `--win escort --goal CX,CY --route "CX,CY;.." --pods "CX,CY;.."` (blue pods added) and `--select CX,CY --delete` set any value (also minutes the stepper does not offer).
- **The skirmish screen** writes the mission over the lower edge of the map's picture (`SkirmishMenu::objective`, `Tr::objective`): "Hold out for 20 min", "Win within 20 min", "Destroy everything", joined with a dot; nothing for a plain map.
- **The human always plays blue** on a custom map: the skirmish screen has no Swap sides on the Custom tab (`SkirmishMenu::select_map`) and `Game::start_skirmish` ignores a side passed for one (`--side` too). A map's maker builds the mission around the blue side; every rule below is the human's.
- **Countdown** = the original's own level data, header words 65 / 66 / 67 (enabled, minutes 1-99, end mode; docs/ai.md `0x42ebd0`), which the sim and the HUD clock already handled for the campaign: mode 0 **hold out** (0:00 = victory), mode 1 **time limit** (0:00 = defeat: win before it), mode 2 **reinforcements**: nothing ends at 0:00, the human gets what the map's REINFORCEMENT record holds (`World::custom_reinforcements`): the message "Reinforcements have arrived", credits and research (no cap, like the campaign's), aircraft into the stock, and one carryall per landing point with the squad the maker chose (like the campaign's scripted carryalls, which bring fixed squads; it costs no stock and leaves the game's squad cycle alone). The original's 20 / 10 / 5 / 2 minute messages show too. The ordinary win and lose rules stay on while it runs (losing the last headquarters loses a hold-out map).
  **Differs from the exe:** the exe has no reinforcement record: mode 2 pays only missions 7, 12 and 17, from its code; OpenSOW pays a custom map what its REINFORCEMENT record holds.
- **Win rule**: no enemy headquarters (default, the original's usual rule) or **nothing of the enemy left**: no unit and no building, the rule the exe ties to missions 4, 8, 13, 16, 21 and 22 (`Objective::destroy_all`, `World::check_outcome`).
  **Differs from the exe:** for a custom map an Advancer alone does not count (its headquarters gone, it cannot fight and may hover out of every gun's reach); the campaign's rule is unchanged.
- **Escort** (win rule 2): blue's pods (kind 46, the Rogon bomb of missions 3 and 8; placed from the Units tab) walk the route by script, waypoint by waypoint, then to the goal; the first pod in the goal's 3x3 cells wins, losing every pod loses (`World::convoy`, `step_custom_convoy`, `check_outcome`; docs/ai.md).
  **Differs from the exe:** the exe ties its convoy to missions 3 and 8 and its computer then uses its unit-then-headquarters pick; OpenSOW runs a convoy on any custom map with the escort rule, and the computer plays against it with `sim/escort_ai.rs` (below).
  Like the missions' pods they walk at 7 px/s (the walk mode of missions 3 / 8), take no move or attack orders, and are re-ordered whenever their path runs out; within one cell of a waypoint a pod goes on to the next. Losing the last blue headquarters still loses, and blue may do without one (`problems`: an escort map needs blue pods and a goal instead; the editor lets blue's last headquarters be deleted on an escort map, `Editor::remove_sel_keeping`); the enemy's headquarters do not decide it. A countdown combines: Time limit = deliver before 0:00. The computer plays against the convoy (`sim/escort_ai.rs`, `World::ai_escort`, not in the exe): it knows the route and goal, as the player does. Its idle ground units (not anti-air, not the Advancer) **strike** a pod within reach; a share **guards the goal** (nearest to the goal first; guards only leave it for a pod within 8 cells); every other unit **intercepts**: it walks to the first cell of the lead pod's remaining way (sampled every 2 cells) that it reaches with time to spare (straight-line distance x 1.35 at its own walking speed against the pods' 7 px/s, plus a margin), spread over free cells, and **waits there** until a pod comes within reach; one that cannot get ahead anywhere follows the lead pod. Roles are kept per unit (`World::escort_roles`, saved) so the guard keeps its size; units it holds stay out of the usual strategy.

  | | strike reach (cells) | goal guard | intercept (margin) | else |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Easy | 12 | none | no | waits where it stands |
  | Normal | 18 | 25 % | yes (10 s) | |
  | Hard | 24 | 30 % | yes (6 s) | |
  | Brutal | 30 | 30 % | yes (4 s) | also calls back units busy with the player's buildings |

  Easy waits rather than falling back to its usual strategy, which sends every unit at the nearest player unit (often a pod) and played harder than Normal. Headless on the demo convoy map (three undefended pods, no blue headquarters, three green flamers): Easy lets the convoy through (victory after 22520 ticks), Normal posts two flamers at the second waypoint and one at the goal and stops it (defeat at 15120), Hard 11820, Brutal 11080. `--report` prints the roles. Distances ignore detours beyond the allowance, so a post behind a long cliff can be late. The goal is not shown in the game (the player finds out where the convoy goes by following it); only the editor draws it. In the editor the map shows the route as dotted legs from every blue pod through the waypoints to the goal (orange where the convoy cannot walk), waypoints as rings and the goal as its 3x3 zone; a goal or waypoint must be a cell a unit can stand on (refused otherwise, green / red under the pointer), and the checks card warns "The convoy cannot walk to waypoint N / the goal" (eight-neighbour reachability over `Editor::walkable`: the game's wave tries a diagonal exactly when the cells beside it are taken). Not done: the goal on the minimap; the computer's air forces and carryalls do not aim at the convoy.
